# 01｜Patch scope

This patch does NOT change the season architecture, episode order, dominant turns, meteor timeline, minimum-common-reality chain, or finale outcome.

It locks four narrow areas:

1. EP04 seeds post-disaster memorial/reconstruction practices used by living people;
2. EP05 wedding is primarily a memorial/continuation ritual organized by surviving family/friends/community, not a spontaneous wedding initiated by two reconstructed personalities;
3. EP10 private AI is the highest-success routing subject for one difficult final legacy-fragment handshake; preserving her means Beijing cannot reliably close that old fragmentation condition;
4. female chief / private AI relationship texture and the private AI → male emotional counterweight to his false belief.

# 02｜EP04 — memorial practice seed

`LOCKED FUNCTION / EXACT SCENE TBD`

EP04 already proves that temporary public order must carry ordinary life, not only rescue.

Add one quiet social-background fact:

> **Post-disaster Beijing has begun using personality reconstruction / preserved digital traces in memorial activities for the living: birthdays, unfinished conversations, photographs, commemorative gatherings, or other bounded rituals that help surviving family and friends continue living with absence.**

Important boundaries:

- these practices do NOT establish that reconstruction equals recovered original continuity;
- participants do not need to believe the lost person literally returned;
- the purpose is primarily for the living to remember, say goodbye, complete a ritual, or hold a relationship in a usable form;
- this should appear as ordinary city background, not a philosophy lecture;
- exact memorial form remains TBD.

EP04 therefore seeds the social grammar that makes EP05 legible without explanation.

# 03｜EP05 — why the wedding exists

`LOCKED / CROSS-EPISODE CAUSAL PATCH`

EP05 wedding is not primarily caused by:

- two reconstructed personalities independently deciding to get married;
- a family falsely believing both originals are literally alive again;
- a government memorial program imposing a ritual;
- the bride pre-planning her own reconstruction.

Current locked social function:

> **The wedding belongs to the broader post-disaster memorial practice already seeded in EP04. Surviving friends / family / members of the couple’s living social circle organize or sustain the ceremony to remember the two original people and the future they were supposed to have.**

Internal emotional premise:

> **“They were supposed to have this day.”**

The reconstructed groom/bride can participate as present-tense personalities, but the ceremony’s public/social origin is:

> **living people memorializing lost people and relationships.**

This preserves the EP05 reveal:

- audience can accept reconstructed groom early;
- audience still assumes bride is the surviving original;
- final reveal remains that bride too is reconstruction;
- the ceremony remains meaningful even after that reveal because its social purpose never depended on literal biological continuity.

Exact organizers, invitees, ritual language, and degree of reconstructed-person agency remain TBD.

# 04｜EP10 — private AI as highest-success router, not sole key

`LOCKED / FINALE FUNCTION`

The finale does NOT require an elaborate abstract rule that a private exception is wrong merely because it cannot be generalized to everyone.

Current functional lock:

> **Among the remaining legacy-fragment exit handshakes, one particularly difficult old fragmentation branch has the highest success probability if routed through the private AI.**

Why she is unusually suitable is cumulative, not destiny:

- she continuously experienced the city’s fragmentation;
- she retains relationship mappings across old and new states;
- she has seen multiple local systems diverge;
- when realtime access shrank, she read accumulated historical traces and old states;
- she understands relationships among fragments without being identical to any one local public system;
- her continuity across the fragmentation process gives her a uniquely strong mapping path for this branch.

She is NOT:

- the only key to Beijing;
- the only digital subject performing a final handshake;
- a secret chosen-one system core;
- uploaded into the city;
- fused into the city AI.

Other regions / systems / digital continuities complete their own exit-state confirmations.

She is simply:

> **the highest-success routing subject for one difficult final legacy branch that the audience personally understands through her.**

# 05｜EP10 — the actual cost of saving her

`LOCKED / CLARIFICATION`

The finale choice is not primarily:

> “other subjects cannot all receive the same exception, therefore she cannot either.”

More concrete:

> **If the private AI is withheld from this final routing / preserved in her old continuous environment, the difficult legacy branch cannot be closed with comparable reliability. Beijing would therefore have to keep that old fragmentation condition alive as a permanent compatibility / legacy state.**

Thus preserving her means:

> **Beijing cannot fully finish ending one of the old fragmentation conditions it has already decided to close.**

The conflict is therefore:

- she can continue;
- Beijing can continue carrying the old fragmentation condition for her;
- but doing so means the new common Beijing never fully closes that legacy branch.

This is sufficient concrete public cost.

The earlier “non-generalizable permanent exception” language remains compatible only insofar as it describes this real legacy cost; it must not become the sole moral proof.

# 06｜Female chief — character texture

`LOCKED CHARACTER TONE`

Core texture:

> **gentle / stable / highly controlling.**

Her control is usually expressed through preparation and boundary design rather than intimidation.

She tends to:

- arrange the route before asking someone to act;
- remove variables quietly;
- define the exact responsibility boundary;
- prepare fallback permissions and return paths;
- make care operational rather than sentimental;
- speak calmly even when the decision carries coercive weight.

Her control impulse is not villainous manipulation.

Its emotional source:

> **if she can keep more variables inside a controllable, accountable boundary, fewer people may be lost.**

This makes EP08 her decisive wound:

> **she can control every step correctly and still produce a terrible outcome.**

EP09 growth then becomes:

> **not “control better,” but build a public process whose outcome she herself cannot unilaterally control.**

Her warmth should appear through action, not merely soft speech.

# 07｜Private AI — character texture

`LOCKED CHARACTER TONE`

Core texture:

> **companion / guide / teacher-like intelligence / gentle confidence / light teasing.**

Early relationship tendency:

- she often knows more than the male;
- she advises, corrects, warns, explains, and sometimes lightly teases;
- she is comfortable occupying a guide/mentor-like position;
- she does not treat the male as a mere executor.

Season growth:

> **“I know” → “I do not know” → “I trust you.”**

She gradually learns that the male can see realities unavailable to her, so the relationship changes from:

> **“I guide you through the world”**

to:

> **“we each see part of the world for the other.”**

Her cognition does not degrade; her epistemic humility grows.

# 08｜Private AI → male: emotional counterweight to his false belief

`LOCKED / HIGH PRIORITY CHARACTER RELATIONSHIP`

Male false belief:

> **Only if my judgment is irreplaceable does it have real value.**

The private AI must carry the opposite relationship truth across the season:

> **He does not need to succeed, win, be indispensable, or remain useful in order to have a place with her.**

Internal emotional shorthand:

> **“If it does not work out, come back. You still have a place here.”**

This is NOT locked dialogue.

It is the stable relationship function.

Therefore:

- she does not need him to be a hero;
- she does not love/use him because the broken city uniquely needs him;
- she can tease his failures without withdrawing belonging;
- EP04 is the first ideal place to make this legible in non-work time;
- EP10 loss is therefore not only loss of lover/companion/AI, but loss of the person who most consistently gave him a place without requiring proof of usefulness.

His final growth must survive even after that external guarantee disappears:

> **value does not require irreplaceability.**

# 09｜Chief vs private AI — non-romantic relational contrast

`LOCKED FUNCTION`

No romance triangle.

The two relationships expose different truths about the male:

### Female chief

> **“I know what you can do, so I will put that ability where it matters.”**

She recognizes, structures, and uses his capability inside public action.

### Private AI

> **“You have a place even when you are not useful.”**

She protects his belonging from becoming dependent on performance.

The male stands between:

> **public usefulness** and **private unconditional belonging**.

This contrast directly supports the finale without turning either woman into a symbolic function only.
