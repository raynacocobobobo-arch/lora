## Current story structure shorthand

- Default episode format = approximately **2–3 minutes per episode**, with one dominant turn per episode.
- Reveal pace = mechanism early, cost late.
- Pressure escalation = physical danger → conflicting facts → correct decisions create losers → regions actively defend post-disaster interests → city-level governance question → the same principle finally harms the male lead himself.
- No traditional season big bad. Opposition should primarily arise from mutually incompatible but reasonable facts, public consequences and interests.
- Public-action line = case-by-case authorization → female chief realizes this cannot scale → real-world Moment of Grace shows that common action can rest on a smaller shared set of verifiable facts → she abstracts minimum-common-reality reconstruction → escalates to citywide representative/authorization level → city authorizes the search/reconstruction process, not a pre-written consensus → regions reconnect one by one.
- The exact Moment of Grace incident remains `TBD`; EP06 is evidence / prefiguration, not the formal lock.
- Minimum common reality is not lossless: some post-disaster local realities, rights, infrastructure, services, or benefits will lose public effect, be converted, moved, closed, or abandoned.
- EP01 = male lead's largely automated-away work → 302楼 missing from relevant system reality while physically present with residents → little girl makes the fact conflict human → male lead enters despite companion AI probability opposition → building temporarily survives → local gravity direction reverses → he falls toward apparent sky / city exterior → orbital-city reveal via black space / huge Earth → female chief catches him.
- EP02 = female chief formally arrives under contingency mission → city-level unified AI/shared-city coordination layer has severe physical failure → regional/professional AIs continue locally → multiple groups across Beijing make locally reasonable decisions → male/female group handles giant opening bridge while other links fail → male lead gives physical risk window, female chief chooses separation deadline → bridge opens before everyone clears → another island forms → hard pressure becomes system-recovery time versus unequal island survival windows.
- EP03 = critical medicine / medical-supply shipment crosses a fragmented low-capacity route. At a key cross-ring / cross-structure node the shipment is **seven minutes late**; both regional systems can correctly say their own docking side is aligned according to local time / structural zero, while the real interfaces are physically misaligned. The male lead remeasures the true physical window; the female chief creates one narrow authorization; the shipment arrives. Dominant turn: **Beijing has not reconnected, but they successfully make one connection happen.**
- EP04 = workday routine across multiple small cases → cinema as final limited task → enough current facts allow one screening → female chief stays because she genuinely likes movies and has repeatedly watched `Rashomon` → male lead + private companion AI remain → work shifts into voluntarily shared private time. Minimum-common-reality is only a seed.
- EP04 three-person structure = private companion AI owns a long shared past with the male lead; female chief is entering his present and future; female chief cannot directly converse with private AI; no jealousy/competition framing.
- EP05 Wedding Mirror = social/private echo of EP02 islanding → both real bride and groom remain missing → commemorative unfinished wedding uses AI reconstructions of both → audience initially reads bride as real and learns late she is also projection → projected wedding snow may appear → after ceremony a few real local unstable snowflakes appear outside.
- EP05 real snow ≠ Finale winter. It is local, light and unstable, not proof of unified Beijing Standard Time or shared winter state.
- **EP06 = regional winter divergence becomes physically undeniable. One region is in real local heavy snow; another is not in winter. Their still-normal shared environment-exchange system must be actively isolated at both ends in the same physical moment. The two regions' local times cannot serve as a common public synchronization basis. Female chief's AI support is already offline; she fully and temporarily gives this operation's final decision authority to the male lead and manually recomputes as backup. The male lead's private companion AI designs the operation using retained pre-fragmentation calibration + current field measurements, with Earth as the main visual reference and `中央电视塔` as the locked Old-Beijing coordinate on Earth. The two sides execute different local observation conditions that correspond to one physical moment. Isolation succeeds; local clocks remain different. Afterward male lead + female chief + privately perceived companion AI sit under the huge Earth, then companion AI visibly drops out of the external scene.**
- EP06 time principle = **a common physical moment can be measured without yet rebuilding common public time.** This is a Finale precursor, not a substitute for it.
- Private companion AI line = early she sees much of outside city → access gradually shrinks → EP06 still makes a decisive high-value contribution using retained shared-history data, but her external presence visibly fails afterward → home remains relatively intact longer → final cost is abandonment of the companion-AI host station required for full common-reality reconstruction.
- Ending = all reconnected regions again share one valid Beijing Standard Time → unified public winter protocol becomes valid → citywide snow returns. Snow is evidence of restored shared reality, not the mechanism.
- Old-Beijing external reference for EP06 = **中央电视塔**, treated as the story-world preserved geographic reference coordinate connecting Earth Beijing to Second New Beijing. The real-world geodetic/legal status of the tower is not being asserted.
