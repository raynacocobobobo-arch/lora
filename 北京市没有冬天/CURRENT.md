## Current precedence

- Highest-level thematic pair = **秩序与牺牲**. “共同现实” is the A-story mechanism, not the ultimate theme.
- EP09 theme words = **承认｜舍弃｜边界**. Older `不完整｜授权｜独立` remains secondary semantics only.
- Companion-AI access ladder: **she loses the outside world, not herself.** Cognition, personality, memory, continuity and independent judgment remain intact while real-time access to Beijing shrinks.
- Male + companion AI home daily life remains `TBD / NEEDS_REVIEW`. Do not invent fixed domestic routines, private-space dialogue or exact home behavior.
- Finale physical layer: Beijing cannot restore everything. It accepts permanent loss and contracts into a smaller sustainable **最小共同体**. Locked emotional endpoint: **北京已经失去了很多东西，甚至变得比以前更小，但剩下来的人终于重新生活在同一座城市、同一个现在里。**
- Ending public-state chain remains: minimum common reality sufficiently rebuilt → one unique valid Beijing Standard Time → one shared public now → unified winter protocol → citywide snow.
- `Reveal-Public-Action-V0.1.3-AI-HANDSHAKE-MULTI-REGION-FAREWELL.md` narrowly revises the old prohibition on active companion-AI sacrifice: **the private AI may actively complete her own final handshake with the city system AI and knowingly accept loss of continuity, but this does not make the male's moral choice for him.** The male still must choose not to demand a private exception that would preserve her at the cost of the common-city plan.
- The final handshake is **not migration or fusion**. The city system AI does not become the private AI; her original continuity ends after the necessary handoff.
- The male's private AI is **not the only AI lost**. City contraction causes multiple old regional / local digital continuities to end because they cannot all enter the new common city without loss.
- Finale visual direction: multiple departing city regions become visible from the space-city exterior and use region-scale lighting / signage / beacons for final farewell expressions in **multiple languages / cultural forms**. Exact languages, count, media and sequence remain `TBD / NEEDS_REVIEW`.
- The second, third and later farewell lights must imply that other AIs / digital subjects are also ending, not only that empty buildings are being powered down. Individual farewell authorship can remain partly ambiguous.
- `TBD` must not be silently filled. `NEEDS_REVIEW` must not be silently resolved.

## Current character shorthand

- **女科长**: public authorization / procedural responsibility. EP09 takes minimum common reality into higher public process and faces the concrete 302 case.
- **私人伴随 AI**: long-term private cognitive companion, distinct from city system AI. External world access shrinks; independent agency remains intact. EP09 she sees 302 through the male. She has already inferred that the same city-contraction logic may eventually close her own continuity infrastructure; **she does not want to leave**, but the male does not know she has reached this conclusion.

- **城市系统 AI**: distributed, procedural, depersonalized; no evil master AI. Finale handshake with the private AI does not anthropomorphize or merge the two systems.
