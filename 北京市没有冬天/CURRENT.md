# 03｜Current season axis — V3.2

The current season is NOT:

> characters gradually learn that sacrifice is the right answer.

Current locked sequence:

> **EP01–EP06：尽可能全部保存。**
>
> **EP07：第一次在局部案件中承认“全部保存”已经失败。**
>
> **EP08：证明完整保全目标不是一个偶发工程问题，而会产生系统级次生灾害 / 制度暴力。**
>
> **EP09：把 EP07–08 已经证明的事实制度化为最低共同现实。**
>
> **EP10：城市级主动执行新的共同边界；同一原则落到私人 AI 与男主自己。**

Internal structure:

> **被动失血 → 局部保存目标裂开 → 保全悖论系统级爆发 → 制度化新目标 → 主动失血 / 向死而生。**

## Preservation Paradox

> **每个系统都在正确保护自己负责的部分；共同前提破坏后，局部保全会把资源、规则、权限、时间和基础设施锁进不同现实，整体北京反而越来越无法维持。**

Secondary disaster is not mainly AI failure.

It is:

> **the externality of locally correct preservation under broken common conditions.**

# 04｜Three levels of loss

## L1｜Forced Loss — EP01–EP06

- disaster creates incompatible conditions first;
- everyone still tries to preserve as much as possible;
- stopping a rescue / losing a connection is experienced as inability to save everything;
- no one yet accepts a smaller future Beijing as the goal.

## L2｜Local Preservation Break / Tactical Sacrifice — EP07

- hub is actively and irreversibly removed;
- **A + B are still the preservation objective**;
- this is the first local admission that A + B + hub cannot all be saved;
- it changes the set of things that must be preserved, but does not yet define a smaller future community.

## L3｜Institutionalized Boundary / Active Contraction — EP09–EP10

- EP09 turns “cannot preserve everything” into a public governance principle;
- EP10 executes it at city scale;
- people should be migrated / evacuated in advance;
- active contraction targets continuing operation, old links, permanent exceptions, regional public states and some digital continuities, not last-second abandonment of living residents.

# 05｜AI principle-consistency learning arc — LOCKED

This is now a season-long character arc, not finale morality invented at the end.

### EP06 — experience

Private AI experiences that:

- female chief may use her judgment only inside a limited emergency scope;
- public authority cannot turn temporary usefulness into permanent access;
- female chief cannot simply declare her side's time the universal Beijing time;
- a common physical reference must bind both sides;
- male, chief and AI are each constrained by a part of reality / authority they do not own.

Internal lesson:

> **共同规则也必须约束拥有权力的人自己。**

No lecture is required.

### EP07 — internalization through history / traces

Locked line remains:

> **“我看不见外面，所以我开始读你们已经留下来的东西。”**

She combines:

- historical records;
- old Beijing traces;
- long-term memory;
- experience of male/chief disagreement and self-restraint;

into an internal principle:

> **规则如果只在代价落到别人身上时成立，就不是共同规则。**

This is an internal writing principle, not mandatory dialogue.

She first applies it to the hub case.

### EP09 — self-application

She concludes 302 should exit future continuous maintenance, then realizes the same rule may reach her own continuity infrastructure.

> **She does not want to leave.**

### EP10 — consequence

She does not become eager to die.

Instead:

> **she cannot invalidate a principle merely because she has become the subject paying the cost.**
