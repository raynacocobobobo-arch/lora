## Current story structure shorthand

- Default episode format = approximately **2–3 minutes per episode**, with one dominant turn per episode.
- Reveal pace = mechanism early, cost late.
- Pressure escalation = physical danger → conflicting facts → correct decisions create losers → regions actively defend post-disaster interests → city-level governance question → the same principle finally harms the male lead himself.
- No traditional season big bad. Opposition should primarily arise from mutually incompatible but reasonable facts, public consequences and interests.
- Public-action line = case-by-case authorization → female chief realizes this cannot scale → real-world Moment of Grace shows that common action can rest on a smaller shared set of verifiable facts → she abstracts minimum-common-reality reconstruction → escalates to citywide representative/authorization level → city authorizes the search/reconstruction process, not a pre-written consensus → regions reconnect one by one.
- The exact Moment of Grace incident remains `TBD`; EP06 is evidence / prefiguration, while EP07 is the major old-method Failure Beat immediately before that later conceptual breakthrough.
- Minimum common reality is not lossless: some post-disaster local realities, rights, infrastructure, services, or benefits will lose public effect, be converted, moved, closed, or abandoned.
- EP01 = male lead's largely automated-away work → 302楼 missing from relevant system reality while physically present with residents → little girl makes the fact conflict human → male lead enters despite companion AI probability opposition → building temporarily survives → local gravity direction reverses → he falls toward apparent sky / city exterior → orbital-city reveal via black space / huge Earth → female chief catches him.
