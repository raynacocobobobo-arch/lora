# 00｜Purpose

This file locks the visual language for all subsequent scene images so that new generations remain recognizably inside the same project, city, public-system design family, and animation art direction.

Core rule:

> **Canon decides WHAT exists and WHAT is happening.**
>
> **This Visual Bible + approved KV anchors decide HOW that fact is visualized.**

A scene is invalid if either side fails:

- visually consistent but contradicts Canon;
- Canon-correct but visually belongs to another science-fiction world.

# 04｜World Visual Identity

The visual world is:

> **Chinese socialist / municipal retro-futurism inside a colossal orbital city.**

The key feeling is NOT:

- a sleek spacecraft;
- a cyberpunk megacity;
- a NASA-style space station;
- a generic hard-SF industrial yard;
- a luxury sci-fi interior;
- a post-apocalyptic wasteland.

The key feeling IS:

> **Chinese urban public infrastructure, municipal order, transport, maintenance, housing and public-service logic expanded into an orbital megastructure and kept alive for years through repair.**

The city is mature, inhabited, repaired, modified, layered, and operational.

It should feel built by institutions for ordinary life, not designed as a hero spacecraft.

---

# 05｜Spatial Grammar

Preferred recurring spaces:

- railway stations / platforms;
- public observation halls;
- checkpoint and transit-control corridors;
- inter-district connectors;
- maintenance halls;
- logistics corridors;
- public system operations spaces;
- residential / shop / service spaces embedded inside megastructure infrastructure.

### Spatial properties

Prefer:

- enclosed or semi-enclosed monumental civic space;
- huge structural bays;
- repeated columns, trusses, beams, gantries, platforms and service levels;
- clear circulation logic;
- public signs integrated into architecture;
- thick, heavy structural mass;
- long-term modification and repair visible as layers.

Avoid:

- wide-open terrestrial skyline as default background;
- giant natural landscape as visual subject;
- generic open space dock full of cranes;
- random kitbash density with no readable public-space function;
- anonymous spaceship corridor grammar.

---

# 06｜Composition Grammar

Default scene-image composition:

> **2.35:1 / frontal / stable / architectural / large-scale / readable.**

Priority:

1. strict or near-strict central axis when the space supports it;
2. stable architectural perspective;
3. ultra-wide or very wide shot for public-system spectacle;
4. human figures and mobile equipment remain small unless the shot purpose is specifically human action;
5. one dominant spatial idea per frame;
6. visual complexity should remain organized, not chaotic.

The image should first read as:

> **a real municipal space with a clear function.**

Then the abnormal condition becomes legible inside it.

Use dynamic diagonals, tilted cameras, extreme low/high angles, fisheye perspective, or aggressive action framing only when a specific approved shot requires them.

---

# 07｜Material Grammar

Primary materials:

- thick painted steel plates;
- dark blue-gray structural steel;
- old white painted municipal machines / rail vehicles;
- welded seams;
- bolts / rivets / panel joints;
- repair plates;
- exposed pipes and service conduits;
- catwalks and guard rails;
- maintenance hatches;
- thick framed industrial signs;
- practical lamps and work lights.

### Aging

Everything important should feel:

> **maintained, used, repaired, still working.**

Use:

- mild paint fading;
- scratches;
- grime at joints;
- repaired sections;
- replacement panels;
- mismatched maintenance generations;
- accumulated temporary structures that have become semi-permanent.

Do NOT make the city uniformly rusty, derelict, abandoned or dirty.

Do NOT make vehicles and machines glossy-new unless the plot explicitly requires a new object.

---

# 08｜Public Signage System

Public signage is a major continuity device and must remain consistent.

Preferred visual system:

- rigid industrial metal / enamel-like panels;
- visible thickness, frame, joints and fixing hardware;
- blue or dark blue background with white lettering for ordinary municipal guidance;
- dark red / muted red only for warnings, civic slogans, ceremonial accents, or approved special uses;
- Chinese main title; restrained small English subtitle where appropriate;
- simple functional typography;
- signage belongs physically to walls, beams, barriers, platforms and machines.

Avoid:

- fabric banners unless explicitly required;
- floating holographic typography;
- giant neon commercial signs;
- glossy LED-advertising cityscape;
- slogans printed across entire vehicle hulls as decorative concept art.

### Current KV signage material lock

The station slogan / title boards should use the same rigid industrial-material family as the wedding-hall and checkpoint signage.

No soft-banner appearance.

---

# 09｜Color System

Base palette:

- cold blue-gray;
- deep steel blue;
- charcoal / dark metal;
- aged white;
- restrained warm-white practical lighting;
- limited muted red accents.

### Red usage

Red is controlled and meaningful:

- wedding carpet;
- public warning;
- civic emblem / signage;
- selected status lights.

Do not flood the frame with red unless specifically required.

### Time-of-day exceptions

Local time / season divergence may create warm-cool contrast when required by Canon or the shot concept.

Example locked KV-01 behavior:

> **left = dusk / right = night with lights on.**

Even then, the structural material palette remains the same municipal steel system.

Avoid default cinematic orange-teal grading.

---

# 10｜Lighting Grammar

Primary lighting sources:

- public ceiling fixtures;
- platform lamps;
- maintenance work lights;
- practical wall / machine lamps;
- controlled skylight or exterior spill;
- limited warning lamps.

Lighting should be:

- restrained;
- readable;
- functional;
- integrated with the public space.

Avoid:

- excessive bloom;
- lens flare;
- glossy ray-traced showroom reflections;
- dramatic golden-hour spectacle unless the specific shot requires local time contrast;
- arbitrary colored neon.

---

# 11｜Rendering / Animation Language

Target:

> **serious cinematic cel animation with hand-drawn mechanical-background discipline.**

Required tendencies:

- clear line boundaries;
- visible drawn / illustrated edge control;
- 2–3 major cel-shadow steps;
- mechanical detail is dense but structured;
- foreground / middle / background remain readable;
- restrained gradients;
- limited bloom;
- characters simpler than the architecture;
- no photoreal concept-art takeover.

A generated image that looks like a premium 3D sci-fi game screenshot is NOT automatically acceptable even if its object list is correct.

---

# 12｜Municipal Equipment Family

Recurring transport, service and security equipment should look related.

Family traits:

- aged white or light gray painted shell;
- simple robust geometric bodies;
- visible seams and maintenance panels;
- small municipal markings;
- practical wheels / rail bogies / service mounts appropriate to function;
- no exaggerated humanoid mecha silhouette;
- no luxury autonomous-vehicle styling;
- no toy-like cuteness unless a specific service robot requires it.

Equipment should feel procured, standardized, repaired and kept in service by the same municipal system.

---

# 13｜Environmental Storytelling Hard Rule

Every major spectacle frame should contain at least **1–3 signs that ordinary public life / maintenance is still continuing**.

Examples:

- commuters still waiting;
- old white trains still arriving;
- cleaning robots still cleaning;
- maintenance vehicles still moving;
- workers still inspecting;
- shop shutters / service counters still operating;
- logistics still passing;
- automatic doors still following procedure;
- warning systems still correctly announcing status.

Core formula:

> **abnormal city state × normal system behavior = unease.**

The city should rarely behave like a generic abandoned disaster zone.

---

# 14｜Crisis Grammar

The project’s crisis is primarily:

> **loss of shared space + shared time + shared order while systems remain locally rational.**

Therefore spectacle should favor:

- structural misalignment;
- incompatible times / seasons;
- duplicated but mutually incompatible public rules;
- infrastructure still functioning under degraded conditions;
- maintained connections reaching limits;
- orderly machines producing dangerous outcomes through correct procedure.

Avoid default disaster shortcuts:

- explosions as primary spectacle;
- giant fireballs;
- random collapse;
- robot rebellion;
- villain screens;
- generic alarms everywhere;
- crowds screaming in every frame.

---

# 15｜Exterior / Space Usage

Outer space, Earth, orbital structures and celestial bodies are allowed only when they support spatial truth.

Rule:

> **space is context, not automatic spectacle.**

Prefer architecture to frame exterior views.

Do not let a giant planet, sunset, mountains, open sky or cosmic vista overpower the municipal subject unless the shot is specifically an exterior establishing shot approved for that purpose.

A scene that could be mistaken for a generic orbital shipyard is a visual-consistency failure.

---

# 17｜Per-Scene Visual QC

Before presenting any generated scene image, check in this order:

1. **Canon fact:** is the scene factually compatible with CURRENT + relevant Episode Snapshot?
2. **Same world:** does it still look like the same orbital municipal civilization?
3. **Same production:** could it plausibly sit next to KV-01 / KV-02 / KV-03 in the same film?
4. **Composition:** is the spatial function readable and the camera disciplined?
5. **Materials:** heavy, aged, maintained municipal steel rather than generic spacecraft kitbash?
6. **Signage:** same physical municipal sign family?
7. **Equipment:** same white / gray municipal equipment family?
8. **Color / light:** restrained cold steel palette with controlled accents?
9. **Life continues:** at least one credible ongoing ordinary / maintenance action?
10. **Crisis logic:** abnormality comes from this project’s connection / time / order grammar, not generic spectacle?
11. **Reveal discipline:** does the image accidentally expose a later protected answer?
12. **Aspect:** 2.35:1 unless later explicitly changed.

Any major failure means regenerate or revise before advancing the asset set.
