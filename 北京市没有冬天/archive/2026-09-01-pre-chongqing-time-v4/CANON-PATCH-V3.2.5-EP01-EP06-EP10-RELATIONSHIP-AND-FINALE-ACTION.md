# 01｜EP01 — private AI already gives the male a place without proof

`LOCKED CHARACTER RELATIONSHIP`

At series start, the private AI already has a long-standing relationship baseline with the male:

> **He does not need to prove usefulness, indispensability, success, or competence in order to have a place with her.**

Internal shorthand only, NOT locked dialogue:

> **“If it does not work out, come back. You still have a place here.”**

EP04 does NOT create this relationship truth. EP04 is the first episode with enough non-work breathing room for the audience to clearly see something that has already existed before EP01.

Male false-belief logic therefore becomes sharper:

- the private AI already tells him, through the relationship, that value does not require irreplaceability;
- he hears/knows this, but does not emotionally trust it as much as external causal proof;
- EP01 302 gives him a different kind of validation: **if I were not here, reality would have gone differently**;
- that external validation strengthens his false belief even though he already has unconditional belonging at home.

# 02｜EP06 — chief ↔ private AI is a MAJOR RELATIONSHIP TURN

`LOCKED / HIGH PRIORITY`

EP06 is the first episode where the female chief and private AI truly enter the same decision relationship.

This is not a minor institutional contact.

It is a major relationship turn for both characters.

## Female chief’s initial view of the private AI

Not:

> “an exceptionally powerful private system attached to the male.”

More accurate:

> **too emotional / too personally attached / inefficient / carrying too much non-task information to look like a clean public-system intelligence.**

From the chief’s public-operational perspective, the private AI:

- spends attention on the male’s feelings and habits;
- preserves redundant personal detail;
- may repeat checks because she cares about him;
- carries obvious private preference;
- does not optimize herself around impersonal public efficiency.

The chief’s initial suspicion is therefore:

> **emotional attachment may make her unreliable for public action.**

## What EP06 changes for the chief

During the shared-reference operation, the chief sees that the private AI:

- remains openly attached to the male;
- does not erase or deny private preference;
- can still distinguish what she wants from what the joint rule permits;
- accepts a task boundary that constrains her own access/capability;
- does not use usefulness as grounds for permanent public access.

Chief’s State Delta:

> **“Emotional” does not automatically mean “unreliable.”**

More precisely:

> **A subject can have strong private preference and still accept a common rule that binds itself.**

## Private AI’s initial view of the chief

The private AI can initially read the chief as:

> **over-controlled / over-procedural / strongly efficiency-driven / someone who compresses emotion into responsibility and boundaries.**

What EP06 changes for the private AI:

- the chief has enough authority to take more, but does not;
- she limits her own access to the private AI;
- she refuses to turn her local time standard into Beijing’s truth;
- she subjects her own authority to the same boundary she expects others to accept.

Private AI’s State Delta:

> **control does not automatically mean absence of feeling; the decisive question is whether power accepts limits on itself.**

## Relationship result

EP06 chief ↔ private AI:

> **from mutual misreading to restrained recognition.**

Not friendship. Not intimacy. Not recruitment.

This relationship is a prerequisite for:

- chief taking AI’s reframing seriously in EP07 while still retaining public decision responsibility;
- chief respecting access boundaries in EP09 rather than absorbing AI into government;
- chief granting the private AI a narrowly scoped public relay/handshake role in EP10.

# 03｜EP10 — finale failure occurs at the male’s legacy branch

`LOCKED FINALE ACTION`

During the citywide common-state cutover, the male is physically located at one legacy branch that still must complete its exit-side final protocol handshake.

The original public/system AI responsible for this branch cannot complete the final state mapping reliably.

This failure must be the accumulated result of fragmentation / historical-state damage / model-reality divergence, NOT a random last-minute server explosion.

Functional state:

- the legacy branch still exists physically;
- evacuation / transition is substantially complete;
- the branch’s public/system AI retains partial protocol identity/history;
- but its current-state mapping is incomplete or unreliable;
- it cannot prove enough of the present branch state to complete the handshake at acceptable reliability.

This deliberately echoes EP01:

> **system state does not equal total present reality.**

> **male has present reality; legacy system has partial protocol continuity; private AI can relay/map the missing state between them.**

# 07｜Why saving the private AI keeps the old fragment alive

`LOCKED / FINALE COST`

Once the private AI becomes the best available relay for this branch, the characters confirm a real alternative:

> **She can be preserved as the same continuous subject if Beijing does NOT fully close this legacy branch and continues maintaining the old compatibility / fragmentation condition that supports her current continuous environment.**

This remains technically feasible.

But if the branch is not closed:

> **Beijing permanently retains one old fragmentation condition that cannot be fully integrated into the new common state.**

Therefore the choice is concrete:

### Preserve her

- she continues as the same continuous AI;
- Beijing continues carrying this legacy fragment / compatibility condition;
- the common-state cutover is incomplete on this branch.

### Complete the branch exit

- she relays/completes the missing handshake state;
- the legacy branch can close;
- her old continuous operating environment exits;
- her original continuity ends.

This is NOT “save girlfriend vs save entire Beijing.”

Without her, Beijing does not instantly die.

Instead:

> **the city must continue carrying a real unresolved fragment of the old world.**
