# 02｜Issues resolved by Patch V3.2.4

## A｜EP05 wedding causal origin

Resolved:

> wedding is part of an already-existing post-disaster memorial grammar seeded in EP04; living family/friends/social circle hold the ritual for the lost couple and their unfinished future.

Therefore the wedding is not an author-created abstract device with no in-world social cause.

## B｜EP10 public cost of saving private AI

Resolved:

> private AI is the highest-success router for one difficult final legacy-fragment handshake. Preserving/withholding her means Beijing cannot reliably close that old fragmentation branch and must retain the legacy condition permanently.

Therefore the finale does not depend only on abstract fairness or universalization.

## C｜Private AI as sole key risk

Resolved:

> she is one high-value routing subject among multiple exiting digital/local continuities, not Beijing’s unique final key.

## D｜Character texture under-specification

Resolved:

- female chief = gentle / stable / highly controlling; care expressed through boundary design and operational preparation;
- private AI = companion / guide / teacher-like intelligence / gentle confidence / light teasing;
- private AI gives male belonging independent of usefulness;
- female chief recognizes and deploys male capability inside public action.

This makes the two relationships distinct without creating a romance triangle.

> → allow ordinary life
> → self-limit power
> → approve active loss
> → discover perfect procedural control can still produce violence

Patch V3.2.4 strengthens rather than changes this line.

## Private AI

Season line remains:

> broad access / guide position
> → discovers limits
> → accumulates shared time with male
> → learns common rules constrain power holders
> → independently reframes problems
> → accepts same principle when it threatens herself
> → becomes one final routing actor while still not wanting to disappear.

Patch V3.2.4 adds the emotional base:

> **male always has a place with her even when he fails or is not useful.**

This is the strongest private counterweight to his false belief.

# 04｜Episode-by-episode screenplay watchlist — NOT new macro Canon

These are execution requirements, not reasons to reopen architecture.

### EP01

- ensure anonymous 302 signal has realtime interactivity + independent physical evidence;
- seed that 302 survives through a legacy/temporary compatibility condition without explaining EP09 early.

### EP02

- make forced loss physically visible and emotionally legible;
- bride cameo must remain natural and unhighlighted.

### EP03

- seven-minute mismatch must remain a physical/state mapping problem, not merely two clocks differing;
- success should feel concretely worth repeating.

### EP04

- avoid “pleasant essay” pacing: cinema case needs real present-tense resistance;
- seed local operating rhythms diverging;
- seed memorial/reconstruction social practice;
- include one non-work AI→male belonging beat;
- show chief’s care through operational control at least once.

### EP05

- keep wedding social origin legible as memorial activity for the living;
- reconstructed participants may have present agency, but social cause remains memorial;
- private AI should register continuity distinction emotionally, not lecture.

### EP06

- shared infrastructure must have a visible human/life consequence so reference solution is not merely technical elegance;
- one synchronized moment must not imply lasting reunification.

### EP07

- hub should connect to at least one function already familiar from earlier episodes so its shutdown has felt cost;
- no perfect third path.

### EP08

- exact action language must distinguish revoking legal weapon control from physically removing weapon capability;
- platforms can stop lawful active targeting while remaining armed physical assets without verified legal control;
- this preserves the uncontrolled-armed-platform reclassification logic.

### EP09

- private AI’s judgment may influence male/chief but cannot itself be the source of public legitimacy;
- 302 public decision comes from the authorized process;
- allow a small unresolved emotional distance between male and AI after their first same-facts/different-scale disagreement.

### EP10

- show private AI as highest-success router for one legacy branch, not sole city key;
- withholding/saving her must visibly imply keeping that fragmentation condition alive;
- male must have real time/opportunity to try to save her;
- handshake transfers/confirms state, not subject;
- snow remains consequence of one common public now, not reward fireworks.
