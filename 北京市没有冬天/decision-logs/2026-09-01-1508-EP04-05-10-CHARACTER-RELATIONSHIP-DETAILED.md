# 01｜Context

After the ten-episode macro consistency pass, the season was re-reviewed specifically for dramatic rhythm, internal conflict, emotional change, and continuity.

The review identified several possible screenplay risks. The user then explicitly narrowed/corrected the important ones rather than reopening the season architecture.

---

# 02｜User correction — why the EP05 wedding exists

User:

> **“最关键——两个人都已经不是原主体以后，到底是谁决定‘我们要办这场婚礼’？ 是为了活着的人纪念逝去的人和朋友，ep4其实前面可以铺垫一下”**

Decision:

- EP05 wedding is primarily a memorial/continuation ritual for surviving people;
- surviving family/friends/social circle organize or sustain the ritual;
- the social grammar must be seeded in EP04;
- EP04 can show that reconstruction-based memorial practices already exist in ordinary post-disaster life;
- this does not mean reconstruction equals recovered original continuity.

Rejected interpretation:

> two reconstructed personalities spontaneously invent the wedding with no social cause.

---

# 03｜User correction — EP10 routing logic should be concrete, not over-philosophized

User:

> **“EP10 现在有一个更大的哲学漏洞，没那么复杂，由她路由握手是成功率最高的，如果不行北京永远无法彻底结束其中一种旧碎片化条件。”**

Decision:

- private AI is the highest-success router for one difficult final legacy-fragment handshake;
- this suitability is earned by her continuous experience of fragmentation, historical reading, cross-state mappings, and relationship to multiple local realities;
- she is not the only key and not the only final handshake participant;
- if she is preserved/withheld from the routing, Beijing cannot close that legacy fragment with comparable reliability;
- Beijing would have to keep that old compatibility / fragmentation state permanently alive;
- therefore the cost of saving her is concrete and public: one old fragmentation condition remains structurally unfinished.

The earlier “cannot generalize the exception to everyone” logic is demoted from sole philosophical justification to a secondary description of the same concrete cost.

# 04｜User correction — female chief character texture

User shorthand:

> **“女科长温柔 控制欲 玛奇玛+拉尼”**

Canon translation deliberately avoids copying any specific existing character’s dialogue, story beats, visual signature, or proprietary traits.

Locked high-level texture:

> **gentle / stable / highly controlling.**

Her care is operational:

- pre-arranges routes;
- defines boundaries;
- removes variables;
- prepares fallback options;
- uses calmness rather than overt domination.

Her control impulse comes from responsibility, not villainy:

> if she can keep more variables controllable and accountable, fewer people may be lost.

This makes EP08 the correct wound: even complete procedural control can still produce institutional violence.

# 06｜User-provided emotional reference for AI → male

User supplied the following relationship reference:

> **“如果你没能当上王，就回到我身边吧，别担心，就算徒弟不成器，也还是有地方让他待着的。”**

This is NOT copied as final dialogue.

What is locked is its relationship function:

> **the male does not need to win, succeed, remain useful, or prove irreplaceability in order to have a place with the private AI.**

Internal shorthand:

> **失败了也可以回来。**

This is the emotional counterweight to the male’s false belief:

> **只有我的判断无法被替代，它才真正有价值。**

EP04 is the first ideal place to make this visible because it is the first substantial non-work shared time.

# 07｜Chief vs AI — relational contrast, not romance triangle

Locked contrast:

Female chief:

> **recognizes male capability and places it inside consequential public action.**

Private AI:

> **gives him belonging that does not depend on performance.**

Therefore the male’s season conflict contains two simultaneous truths:

- being useful really does matter in public life;
- personal worth cannot depend entirely on being useful.

This distinction is important for EP10.
