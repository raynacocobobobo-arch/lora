# 《北京市没有冬天》Master Canon V3 — Design Spec

- Date: 2026-09-01

## Goal

Create a single series-level master document that absorbs the three chronological conversation records and the current EP01–EP10 canon, then rewrites the series architecture *from the finished macro episodes backward*: theme, world logic, character needs, moral structure, recurring motifs, ladders, payoff debts and episode-to-episode state changes must all reflect what the ten episodes actually became.

1. `最早对话记录(1).md`

4. Current explicit user confirmations after those files

Later confirmation overrides earlier conflicting development. Earlier material remains useful when it explains origin, intent or a still-valid mechanism.

## Primary architectural change

The previous series-level framing emphasized **秩序与牺牲**. V3 keeps that moral axis but places **连接** underneath it as the deeper story engine.

The hierarchy becomes:

1. **连接** — can two entities share enough reality to perform one meaningful joint action?
2. **秩序** — can that connection become stable, repeatable, recognized, executable and accountable?
3. **正确** — what action is factually / operationally valid under current conditions?
4. **正义** — who receives the benefit and who pays the loss produced by a correct action?
5. **正当性** — who has the authority to distribute that loss and why should others accept the decision?
6. **两难选择** — two real values cannot both be preserved without cost.
7. **牺牲 / 代价** — every durable order allocates loss; sacrifice is not automatically noble.
8. **连续性** — preserving information, appearance or function is not necessarily preserving the same continuing subject.

## Hand / Handshake redesign

The V3 document must distinguish but connect:

- physical hand/contact;
- infrastructural connection;
- data/protocol handshake;
- public mutual recognition;
- relational acknowledgement.

`握手` should recur before EP10 as ordinary technical / procedural vocabulary wherever the episode already contains an interface, synchronization or mutual-recognition event. EP10 then converts a familiar technical word into a private visual tragedy.

The document must not claim every earlier episode historically used the exact spoken word; instead it should specify where future EP revisions should naturally seed the term/action without changing episode function.

## Character redesign

### Male
Move beneath “wanting to be needed” to the deeper need:
> **He needs his judgment to touch reality and have consequential authorship.**

His false belief remains that judgment only has value when irreplaceable. His season arc must integrate consequence, delegation, trust and finally giving up the broken historical condition that makes him indispensable.

### Female chief
Move beneath “maintaining order” to:
> **A decision that changes other people’s reality must be executable, accountable and morally owned.**

Her initial limitation becomes the belief that a sufficiently correct, traceable process can ultimately legitimate the public result. EP08 proves that correct and legal procedures can combine into an intolerable outcome. Her finale burden is not finding truth but owning the boundary of the future city.

### Private companion AI
Move beneath “losing access” to:
> **She initially equates connection with the ability to access, understand and predict the world shared with the male.**

Her arc teaches that shared reality can persist through trust even when information access collapses. She remains a complete subject, does not want to disappear, and finally acts independently rather than functioning as a self-sacrificing girlfriend/tool.

- Surface Case

- Order State

- Legitimacy problem

The matrix is a basis for future episode rewrites, not a claim that every exact scene is already final.

## World / system requirements

Retain and integrate:

- mature lunar-orbit Beijing;
- AI first saves the city;
- fragmentation begins physically / infrastructurally;
- local AI systems remain locally rational;
- divergent lived histories grow after physical fragmentation;
- four reality layers;
- unique Beijing Standard Time / artificial winter;
- islanding irreversibility window;
- minimum common reality as the only in-story governance term;
- recovery is not reset;
- digital continuity is not guaranteed by copying.

## Document role

`BEIJING-NO-WINTER-MASTER-CANON-V3.md` becomes the **first substantive creative read after precedence / current entry**, and the default basis for changing any EP01–EP10 episode.

Existing `Series-LATEST`, `Series-Bible-V2.1`, `Series-Ladders-Payoff`, and `Series-Development-Status` remain retained as derived/reference documents and must not independently override V3 or episode Latest snapshots.

## Non-goals

- Do not rewrite EP01–EP10 screenplay scenes in this pass.
- Do not resolve engineering mechanisms that remain genuinely undecided.
- Do not lock final dialogue just because thematic language is now clearer.
- Do not invent a villain or a single definition of absolute justice.
- Do not erase superseded development provenance.
