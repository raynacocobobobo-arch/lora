# 00｜Map principle

第一季不追求“十集十个新奇地方”。

核心方法：

> **反复回到少数熟悉空间，让空间状态和社会意义改变。**

观众真正需要记住：

1. ordinary ring city；
2. 嘉陵江开放城市带；
3. 302 outer legacy district；
4. one large EP02 Trunk Bridge；
5. one small Familiar Lifeline used in EP03/04/08/10；
6. Jialing shared-infrastructure family；
7. rare exterior space。

# 03｜EP01 — 302 / small accidental detachment

## Main space

> **302 outer legacy district + one attached outer maintenance/interface segment**

## Movement

> ordinary ring city → regional transit → 302 public side → 302 service/gravity area → attached outer maintenance segment → space.

## Spatial pressure

The whole episode remains one district-scale route.

Gravity vector instability changes the usable surfaces inside the same maintenance/interface path; it does not send the male through several city layers.

## Cost geometry

Safe-reset Plan B genuinely starts working on the attached outer segment.

Then:

> T0 latent interface damage + prolonged abnormal loading → the attached support/interface loses valid structural connection → the small segment accidentally detaches.

302 remains.

## End-state

- one small outer segment lost/damaged；
- 302 remains in Local Operation / repair；
- male drifts outside and is rescued by the unidentified human hand.

---

# 04｜EP02 — large Trunk Bridge

## Main space

> **Level-1 Jialing Trunk Bridge**

It is visually and physically much larger than the later lifeline.

Carries city-scale traffic/services.

## Topology

It connects two large district sides across Jialing.

Nearby/within the broader connection geography there are smaller service/maintenance structures, but they cannot replace its capacity.

## Episode change

> continued rescue begins transferring damage into adjacent interfaces → last safe traffic clears → trunk connection is released / retired.

No explosion.

## End-state

- major direct route gone；
- long detours and low-capacity alternatives become important；
- the absence remains visible in later Jialing views.

---

# 05｜EP03 — small maintenance bridge becomes useful

## Main space

> **the Familiar Lifeline: physically a small Level-4 maintenance bridge**

It is near the former trunk-route geography but structurally independent.

## Scale

Designed for:

- maintenance personnel；
- small cargo carrier；
- industrial robots；
- emergency limited passage。

Not a rail bridge, not a vehicular public bridge.

## Why it matters today

Other public detours still exist but exceed the medical time window.

This small bridge is the only route that can complete the medical action in time.

## Movement

> medical staging → first bridgehead → narrow maintenance span / Gravity Handoff → second bridgehead → receiving side.

## State change

> engineering connection → first narrow public exception.

Its physical size does not change.

---

# 06｜EP04 — same small bridge becomes part of life

## Main spaces

- same Familiar Lifeline；
- ordinary old-district public space / cinema, in 302 or immediate legacy neighborhood.

## Opening geography

The bridge now has:

- basic schedule；
- priority order；
- maintenance checks；
- a small number of ordinary users。

Residents naturally adapt to Level-1 gravity difference / bridge handoff.

## Movement

> small bridge → old district route → cinema.

## State change

> one-off emergency exception → repeat-use ordinary infrastructure.

302 is shown as continuing repair / limited civic reuse after the EP01 accident, not as “remapped” by EP01.

# 07｜EP05 — ordinary city life

## Main space

> **ordinary district module / wedding venue**

No new macro-zone.

The setting should counterbalance engineering episodes:

- family/friends；
- ordinary public transit；
- residential/commercial environment；
- artificial local day/night rhythm。

Across a view toward another part of the ring/Jialing corridor, another district can visibly be in a different local public-time mode.

No topology change.

---

# 08｜EP06 — shared thermal/environment node

## Main space

> **shared infrastructure in the Jialing utility geography**

One physical node has interfaces toward two local districts.

It is not a separate city.

## Spatial image

> one machine / shared utility physically sits between two valid local operating states.

Two sides can see/measure the same neutral mechanical phase.

## State change

One shared transaction succeeds.

Physical connection remains; common ongoing public time does not return.

---

# 09｜EP07 — Shared Hub in the same family

## Main space

> **Shared Hub inside the same Jialing shared-infrastructure family as EP06**

The audience should feel continuity, not a new secret central complex.

## Spatial relation

Hub has high-order control relations to shared physical assets across A/B:

- bridge groups；
- energy/environment buffer；
- Gravity Handoff；
- cross-district network paths。

## State change

Hub hardware remains healthy but its shared-control role retires.

Physical districts remain; operating topology becomes more local.

# 10｜EP08 — same small Lifeline, bridgeheads become legitimacy boundary

## Main space

> **the exact same small Familiar Lifeline from EP03/04**

## Staging

Main confrontation is primarily around the two bridgeheads:

```text
DEGRADED-SIDE BRIDGEHEAD              MAIN-SIDE BRIDGEHEAD
┌────────────────────┐                ┌────────────────────┐
│ industrial humanoids│                │ one medium quadruped│
│ maintenance tools   │                │ staff / security    │
│ interface equipment │                │ interface equipment │
└─────────┬──────────┘                └─────────┬──────────┘
          │                                     │
          └──── narrow Familiar Lifeline ───────┘
```

The humanoids remain because they maintain the degraded-side bridgehead/interface; removing all of them would close the bridge.

## State change

Physical connection survives.

Institutional compatibility worsens after the robots are programmatically disabled.

The bridge does not become a giant militarized border.

# 11｜EP09 — 302 as a whole lived district

## Main space

> **302, shown beyond the EP01 service zone**

Must show:

- residents；
- ordinary routes；
- public rooms；
- service continuity；
- stable local gravity；
- old but functioning interfaces。

## Spatial point

302 is genuinely viable now.

The question is the future cost of maintaining its special connection stack, not whether it is secretly collapsing.

## State change

> real / occupied / safe / maintainable → migration and transition planning.

No immediate physical separation.

---

# 12｜EP10 — last active connection, not bulk-evacuation bottleneck

## Pre-episode migration lock

Migration begins after EP09.

Before EP10 opens, the majority of 302 residents have already left in batches through a mix of:

- then-active temporary connections；
- longer public detours；
- scheduled low-frequency transfer；
- other routes that are progressively retired as contraction advances。

## Main topology at EP10

> **retained city ↔ Familiar Lifeline ↔ 302**

The small bridge is now the **last active legacy connection**, not the route that carried the whole population at once.

## What still crosses in episode

- last residents / remaining roles；
- maintenance/service staff；
- final service transfers；
- Gravity Handoff；
- local-time/protocol compatibility；
- final cutover work。

## Sequence

1. final occupancy clears；
2. last limited transit/service clears；
3. environment/energy/data moves away；
4. Gravity Handoff unloads；
5. time/protocol compatibility exits；
6. bridge still reports physically healthy；
7. final mechanical connection is released/isolated。

## Spatial payoff

The same small bridge has been:

> maintenance path → medical lifeline → ordinary route → legitimacy conflict surface → last active evacuation/service connector → healthy connection deliberately retired.

# 15｜Screenplay blocking test

Before introducing any new large location, answer:

1. Is this only a room inside an existing district module?
2. Can the action occur at Jialing / 302 / the Familiar Lifeline / shared infrastructure instead?
3. Does the audience need to remember this place in another episode?
4. What explicit connector gets the character here?
5. Does the location state change, or are we inventing a new place only for visual novelty?

Default:

> **reuse place, change state.**

---

# 16｜Visual development test

Every environment concept should label:

- parent spatial asset；
- city-facing / Jialing-facing / space-facing orientation if relevant；
- bridge physical level if a bridge is present；
- current Local Time visual state；
- current gravity severity；
- episode/state version。

This prevents a beautiful image from silently inventing new geography.
