# 01｜Core dramatic question

> **当他们终于有能力把一切恢复正常时，“恢复正常”却已经会杀死男主——私人 AI 还要不要完成任务？**

Everything in EP01 serves this question.

The episode is not primarily about how 302 broke.

It is about:

> **a normal work order that gradually becomes impossible to finish through the normal procedure.**

---

# 02｜Pilot function

EP01 must simultaneously establish:

1. Second New Chongqing as a living bridge-network orbital megacity;
2. T0 as an old wound that has been engineered around, not an event happening today;
3. Local Public Time / Local Gravity Frame / public-local handshake as ordinary survival infrastructure;
4. male + private AI as an established everyday/work pair;
5. one simple maintenance objective and visible safe-window rule;
6. the first concrete shared-reality fracture: `302 LOCAL = CHONGQING PUBLIC + 08:00`;
7. private AI's independent judgment;
8. the series grammar `restore normal != current safest`;
9. the stronger grammar `correct plan != guaranteed outcome`;
10. a final physical-human rescue connection that hands the story into EP02.

Worldbuilding must be embedded in current action. No detached historical prologue and no lore lecture.

# 03｜ACT 1 — A routine work order

## Approx. 0:00–0:55

Second New Chongqing is already in an ordinary working day.

The male travels toward 302 through:

- layered artificial districts;
- bridge / rail / service networks at different elevations and orientations;
- ordinary commuters and maintenance crews;
- the bounded Jialing structural void;
- visible T0 repairs integrated into normal civic life;
- local lighting / service differences that imply districts are not perfectly synchronized.

The city is enormous and strange to the audience, but normal to its residents.

The private AI is present from the beginning.

Their relationship is shown through efficient everyday coordination rather than introduction dialogue.

A small `早安` motif may be planted here as an ordinary daily phrase.

## Task record

The active maintenance record narrows the world into today's job:

> `SECOND NEW CHONGQING / CITY MAINTENANCE`
>
> `T0 AFTERMATH MAINTENANCE`
>
> `TARGET: 302 OUTER-SHELL OLD DISTRICT / GRAVITY SERVICE SECTOR`
>
> `LOCAL OPERATION: ACTIVE`
>
> `COMMON REFERENCE: DEGRADED`
>
> `LOCAL PUBLIC TIME: MAINTAINED`
>
> `LOCAL GRAVITY FRAME: MAINTAINED`
>
> `PUBLIC / LOCAL HANDSHAKE: REQUIRED`
>
> `TASK: GRAVITY REFERENCE RESET`
>
> `SAFE RESET WINDOW: ACTIVE`

Exact UI copy remains `TBD`; these functions are locked.

Before danger begins, the audience understands the normal procedure:

> **handshake → gravity reset → safe exit.**

This task record is not exposition decoration. It is the dramatic measuring stick for the entire episode.

# 04｜ACT 2A — The last node

## Approx. 0:55–1:30

The male enters 302.

302 is not a dead ghost city. It is a long-term repaired wound:

- temporary reinforcement;
- sealed residential/service areas;
- damaged layers;
- exposed structure;
- old maintenance bridges;
- pressure boundaries;
- gravity service hardware.

The male is in a lightweight EVA maintenance suit.

The job initially goes well.

Early nodes pass.

Hardware is alive.

Gravity Controller responds.

Only the final trusted public/local handshake fails.

This creates a clear expectation:

> **one more node and they go home.**

Small gravity-drift signs appear:

- condensed water falls slightly off-axis;
- a loose part slides in the wrong direction;
- hanging equipment is subtly biased.

The male and AI notice but do not panic because gravity drift is the known maintenance issue.

The AI still counts the safe window from the last trusted public reference.

The final handshake does not fail because everything is dead. It fails because both sides cannot establish that the other's state is valid **now**.

The male must go deeper to the old relay and create a current physical bridge.

Role split:

> **male = physical current-state access / execution**
>
> **private AI = protocol / history / reference / model**

Neither can substitute for the other.

# 05｜ACT 2B — Eight minutes

## Approx. 1:30–1:50

The male reaches the disaster-era relay and physically reconnects it.

Private AI retries handshake.

Result:

> **PARTIAL HANDSHAKE**

Enough local safety/time authority returns to produce a trusted local timestamp and fragmented local-system warnings, but not enough to control gravity.

302's damaged system voice comes online in broken fragments:

> `GRAVITY RE-LOCK...`
>
> `SAFE WINDOW...`
>
> `EXPIRED...`

Then, for the first time, both trusted references are visible together:

> `CHONGQING PUBLIC`
>
> `302 LOCAL`
>
> **`+08:00`**

No long explanation.

The dramatic fact is:

> **they are not approaching the danger window; they are already eight minutes late.**

Time does not cause gravity failure.

Correct causal chain:

> known post-T0 gravity instability exists
> → public/local trusted time mapping became stale
> → crew misjudged where the known reset/re-lock cycle already was
> → partial handshake reveals the true local position in that cycle.

A small maintenance object falls **upward**.

The maintenance story becomes an evacuation story.

---

# 06｜ACT 2C — Escape while the AI keeps solving

## Approx. 1:50–2:20

The male immediately withdraws.

Gravity does not perform one arbitrary 180-degree flip.

It enters a re-lock / vector-instability cascade:

> floor biases sideways
> → wall becomes usable ground
> → debris changes direction
> → old supports take abnormal loads
> → previous routes disappear
> → the outer-hull side becomes the dominant fall direction.

The action rhythm is:

> **each successful move buys only a few seconds before the environment permanently changes again.**

The same 302 damage shown earlier now participates in the story.

Male and AI operate on two synchronized fronts:

- male physically escapes changing geometry;
- private AI continues completing the remaining handshake over the physical bridge he already established.

The audience keeps one clear hope:

> **if the AI finishes the handshake, they can restore gravity.**

The male crosses toward the large gravity-maintenance / outer-hull service volume.

Pressure boundary conditions worsen; EVA visor seals.

He begins literally falling toward the old ceiling / outer hull.

---

# 07｜ACT 3 TURN — They finally can fix it

## Approx. 2:20–2:38

At the worst physical moment:

> `HANDSHAKE COMPLETE`
>
> `GRAVITY CONTROL: AVAILABLE`

This is the episode's largest false victory.

From the opening task record, the promised sequence was:

> handshake complete → restore gravity → finish task.

The male asks/orders:

> **restore gravity.**

The private AI does not execute.

She now sees the current physical state:

- male displaced under abnormal gravity;
- debris and heavy equipment redistributed;
- temporary structures carrying load in the abnormal direction;
- immediate nominal restoration would reverse all of those loads at once.

The male would likely die.

## Single dominant turn

The episode's one dominant turn is:

> **the goal changes from “restore gravity now” to “do not restore yet; reach a safe reset position first, secure the male, then restore gravity and finish the original task.”**

The AI is not choosing permanent abnormal gravity.

She proposes a complete Plan B with a normal end-state.

This is the first major proof of her independent agency: she can refuse the male's immediate instruction when the current physical state makes it lethal.

---

# 08｜ACT 3A — Use the wrong gravity to reach the right place

## Approx. 2:38–2:58

The AI identifies one external/outer-hull emergency safe-reset position.

Exact engineering name remains `TBD`.

Locked function:

- can physically secure a worker;
- is suitable for emergency gravity-frame restoration;
- once the male is secured there, the AI can restore nominal gravity and finish the maintenance job.

The objective becomes singular:

> **reach the safe reset point.**

The abnormal gravity that created the crisis becomes the only usable path toward it.

The male stops fighting the vector and starts using it.

He deliberately releases from the old orientation and falls toward the outer hull.

The AI provides route, timing and minimal EVA attitude correction.

The male supplies current physical facts from the changing environment.

# 09｜ACT 3B — Genuine success

## Approx. 2:58–3:18

The male reaches the safe reset point.

He does not miss.

He does not slip.

The emergency recovery system actually catches and secures him.

Recovery begins.

He is genuinely moving back toward a safe fixed state.

Private AI prepares:

> `RESTORE NOMINAL`

For the first time since the `+08:00` reveal, the opening work order is about to end normally again.

The emotional tone should be ordinary, not triumphant.

A simple work-like line such as:

> **“好了。”**

may remain a dialogue candidate.

---

# 10｜ACT 3C — The old wound changes reality

## Approx. 3:18–3:28

A low structural failure begins behind the safe point.

No new explosion.

No AI error.

No random tether failure.

A latent T0 meteor-impact fracture in the safe point's structural load path has been propagating under the prolonged abnormal-direction load.

It finally crosses the remaining support.

The **supporting structure itself** tears free.

Therefore:

> the male succeeded;
> the recovery mechanism succeeded;
> the AI's Plan B was valid;
> **then a new physical fact changed the state.**

This is Cost after solution, not a second dominant turn.

The AI still has nominal gravity control and recalculates.

The best action remains:

> **do not restore.**

Meaning shift:

- first no-restore = delay restoration until safe point;
- second no-restore = the safe point no longer exists as a safe physical reference, and restoration still cannot return the male without creating lethal reverse loads.

This establishes:

> **方案正确 ≠ 结果保证。**
>
> **现场新事实有最后一票。**

# 11｜ACT 4 — Leaving the city

## Approx. 3:28–3:43

The male and detached structure continue outward under the remaining Local Gravity Frame acceleration.

At the effective frame boundary:

> artificial acceleration ends;
> acquired velocity remains.

So:

> **falling upward becomes inertial drift into space.**

No vacuum-suction logic.

The audience now sees the ordinary opening city from outside:

- layered hull;
- Jialing structural canyon;
- bridges above;
- bridges below;
- more bridges farther away;
- multi-orientation districts;
- distant Earth as secondary scale reference.

The reveal is not “surprise, they were in space.”

It is:

> **the city that felt like an ordinary workplace at the beginning is now physically too far away to reach.**

The megacity scale finally becomes a direct dramatic cost.

---

# 12｜ACT 4A — Everything works, but it is not enough

## Approx. 3:43–4:03 direction

The action rhythm abruptly slows.

Private AI performs only the remaining useful EVA actions:

1. stop/reduce uncontrolled rotation — succeeds;
2. reduce outbound relative velocity — succeeds.

The suit is not a return vehicle.

Remaining delta-v is insufficient to go home.

This is stronger than a chain of broken rescue devices:

> **every action still available works; none is large enough to solve the whole problem.**

The AI searches for a confirmed return/rescue path.

No executable route is available to her.

This opens her first major knowledge wound:

> **a correct judgment can improve survival and still fail to guarantee a good outcome.**

---

# 13｜ACT 4B — AI capability descent

As incident damage / increased life-support load makes shared suit power critical, the AI begins shutting down capability layers:

- broad environment modeling;
- external search;
- gravity modeling;
- high-rate trajectory simulation;
- other high-compute functions.

She remains present after those functions go dark.

Meaning:

> **first she loses the ability to keep finding answers; only later does she lose the ability to stay present.**

If the male asks whether there is another way, this is the first legitimate opening for her `我不知道` wound.

# 14｜ACT 4C — Good morning / good night

Eventually the remaining competition is effectively:

> private AI local core
> vs
> life support + minimum rescue beacon.

The AI is not a battery.

Her shutdown simply stops drawing from the shared suit power bus and leaves maximum remaining power to the male's survival systems.

She has no return plan left.

Staying online now means only staying with him, while shortening the time in which another rescuer might find him.

The male does not want her to shut down.

No long explanation.

Approved final dialogue direction:

> **“我很抱歉。”**
>
> **“祝你早安……晚安。”**

The opening `早安` routine therefore changes meaning:

- beginning: an ordinary workday greeting;
- ending: two unsynchronized times, uncertain waking/sleeping, and uncertainty over whether there will be another ordinary day together.

Then:

> `AI LOCAL CORE: SHUTDOWN`
>
> `LIFE SUPPORT: PRIORITY`
>
> `RESCUE BEACON: ACTIVE / MINIMUM`

No dialogue after this point.

This is a local operational shutdown, not a locked permanent-continuity death.

# 17｜Character progression

## Male

Opening condition:

> professional confidence that a correct process + skilled execution can complete the job.

EP01 pressure reinforces his future false belief because his physical presence truly is necessary to establish the current trusted bridge and execute through changing geometry.

But the ending also contradicts total self-sufficiency:

> after every system and personal skill is exhausted, he survives because an unknown human physically reaches him.

This opens the season tension around:

> **只有我的判断无法被替代，它才真正有价值。**

## Private AI

Opening condition:

> mature companion + broad knowledge + belief that enough information/control can produce a correct answer.

Dominant turn:

> independently refuses an immediately lethal “normal” action.

Payoff:

> her Plan B is correct and genuinely begins to work.

Cost:

> a new physical fact still defeats the outcome.

End:

> she moves from broad competence → reduced capability → no confirmed answer → only presence → voluntary local shutdown for male survival.

This is the first wound of:

> **我知道 → 我不知道 → 我相信你。**

# 18｜Episode architecture check

## Cold Open

Ordinary city + active task record.

## Pressure

Routine task mostly succeeds → final trusted handshake fails → partial handshake reveals `+08:00` and expired safe window → gravity instability forces evacuation.

## Single Dominant Turn

Full gravity control becomes available, but private AI refuses immediate nominal restoration and changes the goal to safe-point-first / restore-second.

## Payoff / Progress

The pair uses abnormal gravity to reach the safe reset point; the male is genuinely secured and recovery starts.

## Cost after solution

Latent T0 structural damage invalidates the safe point after success has begun.

## End progression

Task becomes impossible → AI prioritizes male survival → EVA aid succeeds but cannot return him → capability layers shut down → AI local core shuts down → physical human hand catches him.

## Cliffhanger

Rescuer identity withheld until EP02.
