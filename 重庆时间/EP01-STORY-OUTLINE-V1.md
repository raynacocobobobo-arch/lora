# 01｜Core dramatic question

> **当他们终于有能力把一切恢复正常时，“恢复正常”却已经会杀死男主——私人 AI 还要不要完成任务？**

Everything serves this question.

The episode is not primarily about how 302 broke.

It is:

> **a normal work order that gradually becomes impossible to finish through the normal procedure.**

---

# 02｜Pilot function

EP01 must establish without lore lecture:

1. Second New Chongqing as a living modular ring-shipyard city;
2. T0 as an old engineered-around wound;
3. Local Public Time / Local Gravity Frame / handshake as ordinary survival infrastructure;
4. male + private AI as established everyday/work pair;
5. one simple maintenance objective and visible safe-window rule;
6. first concrete shared-reality fracture: `302 LOCAL = CHONGQING PUBLIC +08:00`;
7. private AI independent judgment;
8. `restore normal != current safest`;
9. `correct plan != guaranteed outcome`;
10. final physical-human rescue connection into EP02.

Worldbuilding enters only through current action.

# 03｜ACT 1 — A routine work order

## Approx. `0:00–0:55`

Second New Chongqing is already in an ordinary working day.

The male travels toward 302 through a **finite, readable ring city**:

- ordinary district streets inside large district modules;
- city bridges / rail / service connections of clearly different physical scales;
- commuters and maintenance crews;
- glimpses toward the finite Jialing open urban/public corridor;
- visible T0 repairs integrated into normal life;
- local lighting/service differences suggesting imperfect synchronization.

Do **not** require:

- layered artificial districts stacked without clear geography;
- bridges above/below without limit;
- different ordinary districts rotated dramatically relative to one another.

The city is strange to the audience, normal to residents.

Private AI is present from frame one.

Their relationship appears through efficient everyday coordination.

A small `早安` motif may be planted.

## Task record

> `SECOND NEW CHONGQING / CITY MAINTENANCE`
>
> `T0 AFTERMATH MAINTENANCE`
>
> `TARGET: 302 OUTER LEGACY DISTRICT / GRAVITY SERVICE`
>
> `LOCAL OPERATION: ACTIVE`
>
> `COMMON REFERENCE: DEGRADED`
>
> `LOCAL PUBLIC TIME: MAINTAINED`
>
> `LOCAL GRAVITY FRAME: MAINTAINED`
>
> `PUBLIC / LOCAL HANDSHAKE: REQUIRED`
>
> `TASK: GRAVITY REFERENCE RESET`
>
> `SAFE RESET WINDOW: ACTIVE`

Exact UI copy remains `TBD`; functions are locked.

Audience understands:

> **handshake → gravity reset → safe exit.**

The task record is the dramatic measuring stick for the whole episode.

# 04｜ACT 2A — The last node

## Approx. `0:55–1:30`

The male enters 302.

Spatial lock:

> **302 is an outer legacy district module cluster: one side faces the city, the other leads toward its own service/gravity/outer attached maintenance structures.**

It is not a ghost city and not a multi-layer descent.

Visible condition:

- temporary reinforcement;
- sealed or repaired local areas;
- exposed older structure;
- small maintenance connections;
- pressure boundaries;
- Gravity Service hardware.

He wears a lightweight EVA maintenance suit.

The job initially goes well:

- early nodes pass;
- hardware alive;
- Gravity Controller responsive;
- only final trusted public/local handshake invalid.

Expectation:

> **one more node and they go home.**

Small gravity-drift signs:

- condensation slightly off-axis;
- loose part slides wrong way;
- hanging equipment subtly biased.

The AI still counts the safe window from last trusted public reference.

The final handshake fails because both sides cannot establish that the other's state is valid **now**.

The male must move within the same 302 district into the old relay / Gravity Service area and create a current physical bridge.

Role split:

> **male = physical current-state access / execution**
>
> **private AI = protocol / history / reference / model**

# 05｜ACT 2B — Eight minutes

## Approx. `1:30–1:50`

Male reaches disaster-era relay and reconnects it.

Private AI retries handshake.

Result:

> **PARTIAL HANDSHAKE**

Enough local safety/time authority returns for trusted timestamp/warning; not enough for gravity actuation.

Fragmented warnings:

> `GRAVITY RE-LOCK...`
>
> `SAFE WINDOW...`
>
> `EXPIRED...`

Trusted comparison:

> `CHONGQING PUBLIC`
>
> `302 LOCAL`
>
> **`+08:00`**

Meaning:

> **they are already eight minutes late.**

Correct causal chain:

> known post-T0 gravity vulnerability
> → trusted public/local time mapping stale
> → crew misjudged cycle position
> → partial handshake reveals actual local position in that cycle.

Time does not cause gravity failure.

A small maintenance object falls upward.

Maintenance becomes evacuation.

---

# 06｜ACT 2C — Escape while AI keeps solving

## Approx. `1:50–2:20`

Male withdraws toward 302's outer service/interface direction.

No arbitrary single 180° flip.

Gravity enters vector-instability cascade:

> floor biases sideways
> → wall becomes usable ground
> → debris changes direction
> → supports take abnormal loads
> → old route disappears
> → outer-facing side becomes dominant fall direction.

Every successful move buys only seconds before geometry changes again.

Male and AI work two fronts:

- male escapes current geometry;
- AI completes remaining handshake over the physical bridge already established.

Audience hope:

> **if handshake finishes, gravity can be restored.**

The male reaches the outer part of 302's Gravity Service / small attached maintenance-interface route.

Pressure boundary conditions worsen; visor seals.

He begins literally falling toward the outer-facing side.

---

# 07｜ACT 3 TURN — They finally can fix it

## Approx. `2:20–2:38`

At the worst physical moment:

> `HANDSHAKE COMPLETE`
>
> `GRAVITY CONTROL: AVAILABLE`

False victory.

Opening promise was:

> handshake complete → restore gravity → finish task.

Male asks/orders restore.

AI does not execute.

She now sees:

- male displaced under abnormal gravity;
- debris/heavy equipment redistributed;
- temporary structures loaded in abnormal direction;
- immediate nominal restoration would reverse those loads at once.

Male would likely die.

## Single dominant turn

> **goal changes from “restore gravity now” to “do not restore yet; reach a safe reset position first, secure male, then restore and finish original task.”**

AI is not choosing permanent abnormal gravity.

Plan B has a normal end-state.

This is her first major independent-agency proof.

# 08｜ACT 3A — Use wrong gravity to reach the right place

## Approx. `2:38–2:58`

AI identifies one emergency safe-reset / securing position on the **small outer attached maintenance/interface segment** connected to 302.

Exact engineering name remains `TBD`.

> **reach the safe reset point.**

# 09｜ACT 3B — Genuine success

## Approx. `2:58–3:18`

Male reaches the safe reset point.

He does not miss or slip.

Emergency recovery system catches and secures him.

Recovery begins.

He genuinely moves toward a safe fixed state.

AI prepares:

> `RESTORE NOMINAL`

The original work order appears about to end normally again.

Emotional tone: ordinary, work-like, not triumphant.

`好了。` remains a candidate line, not locked.

# 10｜ACT 3C — The old interface wound changes reality

## Approx. `3:18–3:28`

A low structural/interface failure begins inside the load path attaching this small outer segment to 302.

No explosion.

No AI error.

No random tether failure.

T0 left a latent fracture/interface scar that was not fully observable. Prolonged abnormal-direction load propagates it.

Then:

> **the small attached maintenance/interface segment itself loses valid structural connection to 302 and accidentally detaches.**

Therefore:

> male succeeded;
> recovery mechanism succeeded;
> AI Plan B was valid;
> **then a new physical fact changed the state.**

302 itself remains connected to the city.

This is Cost, not second dominant turn.

AI still has nominal gravity control and recalculates.

Best action remains:

> **do not restore.**

Meaning shift:

- first no-restore = delay until safe point;
- second no-restore = safe point/attached segment is no longer connected to the city, and restore still cannot safely return male.

This establishes:

> **方案正确 ≠ 结果保证。**
>
> **现场新事实有最后一票。**

# 11｜ACT 4 — Leaving the city

## Approx. `3:28–3:43`

Male + detached small segment continue outward with acquired motion.

At Local Gravity Frame boundary:

> artificial acceleration ends;
> acquired velocity remains.

So:

> **falling upward becomes inertial drift into space.**

No vacuum suction.

External city reveal must follow Spatial V2:

- readable curved ring-city mass;
- distinct district-module clusters;
- clearly different bridge scales around the Jialing urban corridor;
- visible repaired/used surfaces;
- distant Earth as secondary reference.

Do not show:

- infinite layered hull;
- bridges above/below forever;
- multi-orientation district maze.

The reveal is not “surprise, they were in space.”

It is:

> **the ordinary workplace from the beginning is now physically too far away to reach.**

# 12｜ACT 4A — Everything works, but not enough

## Approx. `3:43–4:03 direction`

Pacing sharply slows.

AI performs only remaining useful EVA actions:

1. reduce uncontrolled rotation — succeeds;
2. reduce outbound relative velocity — succeeds.

Suit is not a return vehicle.

Remaining delta-v insufficient.

> **every action still available works; none is large enough to solve the whole problem.**

AI searches for confirmed return/rescue path.

No executable route available to her.

This opens first knowledge wound:

> **a correct judgment can improve survival and still fail to guarantee good outcome.**

# 13｜ACT 4B — AI capability descent

Incident damage/increased life-support load makes shared power critical.

AI shuts down capability layers:

- broad environment modeling;
- external search;
- gravity modeling;
- high-rate trajectory simulation;
- other high-compute functions.

She remains present after these go dark.

Meaning:

> **first she loses ability to keep finding answers; only later does she lose ability to stay present.**

If male asks whether another way exists, this is first legitimate opening for `我不知道` wound.

# 14｜ACT 4C — Good morning / good night

Remaining competition becomes:

> private AI local core
> vs
> life support + minimum rescue beacon.

AI is not a battery.

Shutdown stops her draw from shared suit power.

She has no return plan left.

Staying online now only means staying with him while shortening rescue time.

Male does not want shutdown.

> **“我很抱歉。”**
>

# 16｜But / Therefore causal chain

> 男主和 AI 只想完成普通重力维修，**但是** final trusted handshake 无法建立当前状态，
> **所以**男主必须进入同一302城区的旧 relay/service area；
> **但是** Partial Handshake 一恢复，他们发现已经快八分钟，safe window 早过，
> **所以**男主立即撤离，同时 AI 继续完成控制握手；
> **但是** Full Handshake 成功时，当前几何使“恢复正常重力”变成致命动作，
> **所以** AI 拒绝恢复，提出先到外缘安全重置位置；
> **但是**他们真的到达并开始成功回收后，T0 旧伤使**小型附属接口段本身断接**，
> **所以** AI 再次选择不恢复，男主与小段结构漂入太空；
> **但是** EVA 能力只能减速不能返航，
> **所以** AI 最后把运行功耗让给维生；
> **但是**就在男主将失去意识时，
> **一只人的手抓住了他。**

If screenplay cannot preserve this chain, revise screenplay rather than add lore.

# 17｜Character progression

## Male

Opening:

> professional confidence that correct process + skilled execution completes job.

EP01 reinforces his future false belief because his physical presence is genuinely necessary.

Ending contradicts total self-sufficiency:

> after system + skill are exhausted, an unknown human physically reaches him.

This opens:

> **只有我的判断无法被替代，它才真正有价值。**

## Private AI

Opening:

> mature companion + broad knowledge + belief enough information/control can produce correct answer.

Dominant turn:

> independently refuses lethal “normal” action.

Payoff:

> Plan B correct and genuinely starts working.

Cost:

> new physical fact defeats outcome.

End:

> broad competence → reduced capability → no confirmed answer → presence only → voluntary local shutdown.

First wound of:

> **我知道 → 我不知道 → 我相信你。**

Full control available → AI refuses immediate nominal restoration → safe-reset-first / restore-second.
