# 《重庆时间》SPATIAL ENVIRONMENT CANON PATCH V2.1 — ASTEROID CORE

- Updated: 2026-09-02
- Status: `LOCKED / ACTIVE HIGH-PRIORITY SPATIAL PATCH`
- Project: **《重庆时间》**
- City: **第二新重庆市**
- Scope: city macro-form, central-core geometry, permanent ring relation, exterior orientation, material/structural reading, Jialing relation
- Supersedes: any prior active or execution wording that describes the center of Second New Chongqing as primarily hollow / empty / open torus void

---

# 00｜Highest-level correction

> **第二新重庆不是一座“中间完全中空”的环形太空城。**

New locked macro-form:

> **第二新重庆是一座以一颗被捕获、稳定化并工程化固定的小行星为中央核心，在其外围构建永久环形船坞骨架、城区模块与多尺度连接体系的模块化轨道城市。**

Visual shorthand:

> **asteroid-centered ring-shipyard city**

The city still reads as an irregular toroidal / ring-shipyard structure, but the torus is built **around a real central asteroid core**, not around an empty hole.

---

# 01｜Central asteroid core

## CENTRAL ASTEROID CORE

The geometric/visual center of Second New Chongqing contains one large captured asteroid.

Locked functions:

- central structural / docking reference body;
- long-term construction anchor and industrial foundation;
- radiation / debris shielding mass where useful;
- raw-material / repair-material reserve where appropriate;
- central service / industrial / logistics support geography;
- major visual identity of the city from exterior views.

Important:

> **The asteroid is not simply a decorative rock floating in the middle. It has been engineered into the city.**

It may be partially excavated, reinforced and wrapped by service structures, but it is not primarily a giant inhabited underground city.

---

# 02｜Asteroid stabilization and structural integration

The central asteroid must be physically credible.

Do not assume loose regolith directly carries the ring-city loads.

Preferred engineering logic:

1. selected coherent rock / metal-bearing body or stabilized captured asteroid;
2. deep drilled anchors / through-body anchoring where geology allows;
3. external containment / reinforcement lattice around major load-transfer regions;
4. engineered central structural collar / dock frame around or through the asteroid;
5. multiple redundant radial dock-truss / service connections extending from the central core toward the permanent ring backbone;
6. flexible / isolated transition joints where different structural bodies need thermal, vibration or displacement tolerance.

The asteroid surface itself may remain partly visible between engineered reinforcement zones.

Visual rule:

> **rock mass + engineered restraint + central dock structure + radial connections**

not:

> bare asteroid magically holding a city together.

Exact number of spokes / radial trusses remains execution TBD.

---

# 03｜Permanent ring relation

The Permanent Ring Backbone remains active Canon.

New relation:

> **The permanent ring backbone surrounds the central asteroid core and is mechanically / infrastructurally related to it through a bounded set of radial structural, docking, logistics and service connections.**

The ring still:

- carries district-module attachment;
- supports long-term expansion and replacement;
- provides circumferential structural continuity;
- remains after districts or legacy connections retire;
- is not cut as the default finale contraction mechanism.

The center asteroid does not replace the ring backbone.

They are complementary:

> **asteroid core = central anchored mass / industrial-support core**
>
> **permanent ring = primary circumferential city backbone**

---

# 04｜Not a centrifugal-gravity torus

`LOCKED`

The new asteroid-centered design does **not** restore Stanford-torus gravity.

Forbidden interpretation:

- ring rotates to produce 1G;
- inward / centrifugal gravity arrows;
- asteroid gravity provides normal city 1G;
- gravity is automatically radial toward the asteroid.

The asteroid's natural gravity is not the normal lived-city gravity mechanism.

Local Gravity Frames remain an independent engineered system.

Therefore:

> **central asteroid geometry, ring topology and artificial gravity remain separate axes.**

# 05｜Center is occupied, but not fully solid

The center is no longer described as a hollow / empty torus void.

Correct reading:

> **中央空间由小行星核心 + 工程化包覆/中央船坞节点 + 径向连接 + 有边界的作业/运输空域共同构成。**

There may still be bounded open volumes between the asteroid core and the surrounding ring for:

- docking;
- cargo movement;
- inspection;
- maintenance craft;
- radial transport;
- structural access.

But the audience should never read the city as:

> a huge empty donut hole.

# 06｜Jialing relation after asteroid-core patch

It is still NOT:

New spatial opportunity:

Ordinary city life still occurs primarily in district modules with:

- housing;
- hospitals;
- schools;
- commerce;
- transit;
- public space;
- service districts.

The central asteroid core may contain industrial/service spaces, but it does not replace ordinary district geography.

# 10｜Material / structural credibility

The asteroid-core structure should visibly distinguish rock, primary structure and habitation/service layers.

## Asteroid body

Possible visual geology:

- dark silicate / carbonaceous rock;
- metal-rich exposed zones;
- fractured but stabilized surface;
- excavated anchor corridors;
- regolith retained only where structurally controlled.

## Primary asteroid restraint / dock structure

Preferred language:

- high-strength steel / titanium-alloy-like deep anchor hardware;
- large external containment rings / lattice frames;
- replaceable inspection collars;
- heavy node structures around radial truss attachments;
- redundant load paths;
- shock / vibration isolation at selected interfaces.

## Ring backbone

Continue using:

- segmented box girders;
- large space trusses;
- circumferential load paths;
- standardized district attachment nodes;
- layered pressure / debris protection on inhabited modules.

Important physics rule:

> The asteroid is not an infinite rigid body. Structural links must allow inspection, redundancy, isolation and differential thermal/elastic behavior.

# 11｜Construction-history logic

The city should read as having been built outward from a captured central resource/anchor body over time.

Suggested historical visual logic:

1. asteroid captured / stabilized;
2. central dock / reinforcement collar established;
3. first radial industrial/service links built;
4. permanent circumferential ring backbone constructed / expanded;
5. district modules accumulated around the ring;
6. bridge network and Jialing public geography matured;
7. generations of replacement and repair created visible non-uniformity.

This construction history is a visual logic, not a mandatory first-season exposition sequence.

---

# 12｜T0 meteor impact distinction

Critical distinction:

> **The embedded central asteroid is NOT the T0 impactor.**

The asteroid core predates T0 and is part of the city's original / long-term construction history.

T0 is a separate meteor / impact event that damaged parts of the city.

Therefore exterior images may simultaneously show:

- stable central asteroid core;
- old ring-city modules built around it;
- a separate historical T0 impact scar on a district / ring / interface region;
- emergency sealing, reinforcement and long-term maintenance traces.

Never imply that the city formed around the same meteor that caused T0.
