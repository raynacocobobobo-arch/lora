# 01｜Core lock

> **302 is not merely one ordinary outer legacy residential district. It is an old industrial–residential–transfer–compatibility complex whose historical origin is tied to the early exploitation of the central asteroid.**

A key canonical subspace is:

> **the internal asteroid mining / excavation opening, which is part of the historical 302 system and one of the physical origin layers of 302.**

302 therefore carries both:

- a lived civic / residential legacy layer;
- an older extraction / logistics / engineering ancestry embedded in the city’s asteroid-core construction history.

---

# 02｜302 is a compound legacy system

302 should now be read as a **compound legacy zone / compatibility group**, not as one homogeneous residential block.

Its functional layers may include:

1. **Asteroid Origin Layer** — early mining / excavation / extraction spaces inside the engineered central asteroid;
2. **Industrial Transfer Layer** — old haulage, material handling, lifting, maintenance, utility and logistics interfaces descended from the extraction era;
3. **Legacy Civic / Residential Layer** — inhabited spaces, public rooms, old services and ordinary life associated with the same legacy system;
4. **Compatibility Layer** — old time / gravity / protocol / service interfaces that remain expensive to preserve;
5. **Outer Service / Space-facing Layer** — the part of 302 relevant to EP01’s outer maintenance/interface route and small accidental detachment.

These layers belong to one historical / administrative infrastructure lineage, but **do not imply that every episode traverses all of them**.

---

# 03｜Continuity reconciliation

Earlier active material described 302 too narrowly as a single outer legacy district with one city-facing side and one space-facing side.

V2.2 corrects that simplification.

The active interpretation is now:

> **302 has an outer lived/service component with city-facing and space-facing interfaces, while the broader 302 legacy system also extends into older asteroid-core industrial origin spaces.**

Therefore:

- EP01 can still occur entirely in the **outer 302 civic/service/space-facing sub-route**;
- the internal asteroid mining opening does **not** need to appear in EP01;
- no EP01 multi-layer traversal is reintroduced;
- the asteroid mining origin gives 302 history and structural ancestry, not a mandatory movement path for every 302 story.

---

# 04｜City-history implication

Second New Chongqing’s growth history should now read roughly as:

```text
captured / stabilized asteroid
→ extraction + engineering exploitation
→ early industrial / logistics / worker-support zones
→ permanent ring infrastructure expands
→ newer civic districts become dominant
→ extraction-linked 302 system becomes a legacy industrial-residential compatibility zone
→ continuing operation accumulates compatibility and maintenance debt
```

302 is therefore one of the places where the city’s **origin labor and origin infrastructure remain physically present**.

It is not simply “a poor old neighborhood.”

---

# 05｜Thematic function

302 now carries the series question:

> **How should a city treat the infrastructure and labor-origin systems that made the city possible, once those systems are no longer efficient enough to justify permanent common support?**

Its political / ethical tension is not:

> “Is this district secretly unsafe?”

It is:

> **“How long must the whole city continue to guarantee a costly legacy reality simply because that reality is historically real, inhabited, and foundational?”**

This strengthens the existing Preservation Paradox without changing the basic ending principle.

---

# 06｜Season use — B + C LOCK

The asteroid mining opening is **not** primarily used as a mid-season twist/reveal.

Its active dramatic use is:

> **B + C = EP09 spatial evidence before the public decision + EP10 final look-back / orderly retirement space.**

## EP09 — spatial evidence before decision

Before / around the episode’s institutional decision, the story may physically enter or visually return to the old asteroid mining opening.

Purpose:

- prove that 302 is a real historical system, not an abstract budget line;
- show the old extraction / transfer / maintenance infrastructure still physically exists;
- show old civic / labor markings, maintenance traces and continuing limited operation;
- let the audience understand that the future guarantee question concerns an entire inherited operating world, not just housing;
- make the withdrawal of permanent special support feel like a decision about the city’s own origin layer.

The space should feel old, severe and still legible as infrastructure — **not secretly collapsing and not staged as a disaster zone**.

## EP10 — final look-back / retirement

The same origin-layer space may return near the end as one of the last spaces to leave retained common operation.

Preferred dramatic behavior:

- machinery powers down in sequence;
- remaining staff / duties clear first;
- old control booth or service lights go dark;
- logistics indicators / maintenance systems retire;
- no explosion;
- no catastrophic collapse;
- the rock, structures and historical traces remain physically present after common support ends.

The emotional read is:

> **the city is not destroying its origin; it is ceasing to keep that origin permanently inside the retained common operating present.**

This directly supports:

> **exit ≠ nonexistence.**
