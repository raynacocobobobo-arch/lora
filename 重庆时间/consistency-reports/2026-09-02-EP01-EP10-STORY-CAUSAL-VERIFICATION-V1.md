> `Trigger -> Goal -> Obstacle -> Choice -> Turn -> Cost -> State Delta`

> `Cold Open -> Pressure -> Turn -> Payoff/Progress -> End Image`

7. is there exactly one dominant turn?
8. does success still leave cost/debt?

# 02｜EP01 — task record / +08:00 / gravity choice

## Trigger

A routine post-T0 302 Gravity Reference Reset cannot complete its final trusted public/local handshake remotely, so the male must physically enter the old relay/service layer while the private AI handles protocol/reference/model work.

## Goal

Complete trusted handshake, restore/re-lock the 302 gravity reference, and leave before the safe reset window closes.

The task record establishes the normal finish state before the story breaks it.

## Obstacle 1

Most of the job works. The final trusted public/local reference does not.

The AI can only use `CHONGQING PUBLIC` plus the last trusted mapping; unverified local timestamps cannot safely authorize hazardous shared actions.

## Pressure reveal

The male establishes a physical bridge to the disaster-era relay.

Partial handshake restores a trusted local safety/time channel but not full gravity actuation.

It exposes:

> `302 LOCAL = CHONGQING PUBLIC + 08:00`

and:

> safe reset window already expired / gravity re-lock instability already active.

This is Pressure, not the dominant turn.

## Goal under pressure

Evacuate while the AI continues completing the handshake over the physical bridge already established.

## Dominant Turn / Choice

Full handshake succeeds and `GRAVITY CONTROL` becomes available.

The obvious normal action is now executable:

> restore nominal gravity.

But current physical geometry has changed: male, debris and structural loads are already redistributed under the abnormal vector.

The private AI independently refuses immediate restore because it would make male survival near-zero.

She changes the goal to:

> **reach a safe reset position → secure male → restore gravity there → finish original task.**

Exactly one dominant turn is present.

## Payoff / Progress

The pair uses the abnormal vector as the only usable route toward the safe reset point.

Male physically executes through changing geometry; AI supplies route/reference/control assistance.

He genuinely reaches the safe point, is secured, and recovery begins.

The Plan B is demonstrably valid and is actually succeeding.

## Cost after solution

A latent T0 meteor-impact fracture in the safe point's load path propagates under prolonged abnormal loading and tears the supporting structure loose.

This is a newly revealed physical fact, not AI stupidity, male error or fake equipment failure.

The AI reassesses `RESTORE NOMINAL`; the best action remains **do not restore**, because the safe point itself is no longer safe and restoration still cannot return the male without reversing lethal debris/load motion.

The fracture is Cost, not a second dominant turn.

## End progression

Male crosses beyond the Local Gravity Frame and continues outward by inertia.

AI successfully stops rotation and reduces outbound relative speed, but available EVA delta-v is insufficient for return.

Power becomes critical. AI shuts down high-compute functions first; later she shuts down the local AI core to maximize life support + minimum rescue beacon.

Approved final dialogue direction:

> “我很抱歉。”
>
> “祝你早安……晚安。”

No dialogue follows.

An unidentified human EVA hand catches the nearly unconscious male; identity moves to EP02.

## State Delta

- ordinary city / task record establishes post-T0 local operation as normal life;
- trusted `+08:00` 302/public split is exposed;
- time and gravity remain independent axes;
- private AI proves independent safety judgment;
- safe-reset Plan B truly succeeds before a new physical fact defeats task completion;
- `restore normal != current safest` and `correct plan != guaranteed outcome` are seeded;
- AI local session shuts down for suit survival, not permanent continuity death;
- unidentified rescuer catches male;
- 302 remains a real legacy district/service node for later use.

## Counterfactual / cheap-answer gate

- no onsite male → no current trusted physical bridge for the failing legacy relay;
- “just use 302 timestamp” → invalid before trusted handshake;
- “restore as soon as full control returns” → lethal under current geometry;
- “keep abnormal gravity forever” → not the AI's plan; Plan B still aims to restore at the safe point;
- “he simply misses the tether” → prohibited; recovery genuinely begins;
- “AI miscalculates” → prohibited; new latent physical fact changes the state;
- “use another rescue drone/device” → no post-space device cascade; EVA assistance is enough to improve survival but not return him.

**Result: PASS at macro story architecture level.**
