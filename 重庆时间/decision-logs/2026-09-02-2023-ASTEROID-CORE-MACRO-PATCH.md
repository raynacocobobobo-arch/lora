# 03｜New macro lock

New city shorthand:

> **第二新重庆 = 中央工程化小行星核心 + 永久环形船坞骨架 + 城区模块 + 多尺度桥网/服务连接。**

The city is an asteroid-centered ring-shipyard city.

The central asteroid is:

- captured before T0;
- stabilized and engineered into the city;
- structurally integrated through reinforcement, dock/collar nodes and radial service/structural connections;
- a major exterior visual identity;
- not a decorative rock;
- not a giant inhabited underground city by default.

---

# 04｜Physics / materials guardrails

To maintain physical credibility:

- loose asteroid regolith does not directly carry ring loads;
- load-transfer zones use deep anchors / through-body or drilled anchoring where geology allows;
- critical regions use external containment/reinforcement lattices and engineered structural collars;
- ring-core links use redundant radial structural/service trusses;
- selected interfaces allow vibration, thermal expansion and displacement tolerance;
- exact asteroid geology, size, mass and spoke count remain TBD rather than invented as hard Canon.

The central asteroid does not provide citywide 1G through natural gravity.

The ring does not rotate to produce centrifugal 1G.

Local Gravity Frames remain independent engineered infrastructure.
