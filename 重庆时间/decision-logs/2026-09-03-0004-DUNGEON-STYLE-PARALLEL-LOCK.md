# 04｜Style characteristics locked

- strong black negative space;
- large aged off-white / gray industrial surfaces;
- simplified illustrated mechanical linework;
- 2–3 hard cel-shadow tiers;
- sparse muted rust-red accents;
- selected red-white industrial module/repair bands;
- visible impact and repair history;
- large in-world municipal/civic lettering allowed;
- low high-frequency detail;
- Seedance-friendly stable silhouettes;
- not photoreal hard-SF;
- not cyberpunk neon;
- not luxury spacecraft design.
