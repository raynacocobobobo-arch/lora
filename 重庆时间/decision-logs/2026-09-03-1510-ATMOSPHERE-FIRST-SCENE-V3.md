# Decision Log — Atmosphere-First Scene Generation V3

- Date: 2026-09-03 15:10 (+08)
- Status: `APPROVED / ACTIVE`
- Scope: environment / scene-atmosphere generation methodology

## What failed before

Earlier environment generations often looked stylistically close to the project but failed production usefulness because:

- the method started from style before fully resolving scene function;
- scene identity depended too much on signage;
- one named technical node was incorrectly enlarged into the whole episode space;
- core-space requests were answered as local rooms/equipment views instead of atmosphere masters;
- Seedance simplification removed detail without distinguishing identity geometry from fragile micro-noise;
- composition family was not explicitly selected.

## What the successful 302 studies established

The successful direction uses:

- whole-space atmosphere rather than local object framing;
- large off-white structural masses;
- large open void;
- strong route / depth direction;
- tiny human scale;
- sparse rust-red repair/civic structure;
- strong directional light with large shadow masses;
- axial / center-symmetrical or near-symmetrical composition when appropriate;
- architecture that already contains the surfaces and structures later action will use.

These are method discoveries, not a new style replacing Oshii Animation Style V1.

## New method lock

> **Canon → shot mode → dominant action geography → scene identity → parent space → narrative affordances → composition → Oshii V1 → Seedance structural compression → generation → QC.**

For `核心空间 / 场景气氛图 / 环境母图 / 大场景`, default to a 2.35:1 wide atmosphere master.

For episode core-space requests, choose the spatial system that carries the majority of dominant physical action/state change, not simply the most prominent named node.

## Seedance refinement

Seedance optimization is now:

> **structural compression**

not indiscriminate simplification.

Keep major silhouette, route, void, structural frames, light-shadow masses and identity anchors.

Merge repeated rails, catwalks, windows, cross-bridges, pipes, lights and distant machinery into fewer stable groups.

> **删噪声，不删身份；合并结构，不削平空间。**
