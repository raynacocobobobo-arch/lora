## Global Constraints

- Never write to `main` during implementation.
- Ring city = permanent ring backbone + district modules + attached service/maintenance modules.
- Main-ring radius direction ≈ 1 km; major interior clear-height direction ≈ 200 m; dimensions are visual/design scale only.
- `嘉陵江` = finite continuous open urban/public corridor inside the ring, not the torus central hole and not an infinite canyon.
- Familiar Lifeline remains physically a small Level-4 maintenance bridge through EP03/04/08/10.
- EP01 detaches only one small 302 outer attached interface/maintenance segment; 302 remains.
- Ordinary district nominal gravity orientation is visually mostly aligned; Local Gravity Frames remain active and become strongly visible mainly at bridges/interfaces/damage zones.
- EP10 migration begins before the episode; the small lifeline is the last active legacy connection, not the sole bulk-evacuation route.
- Do not change the approved EP01 dominant turn, `+08:00` pressure role, Plan-B genuine success, AI shutdown, or final rescue hand.
- Do not introduce new active spatial authorities that compete with V2.
