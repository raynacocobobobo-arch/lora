> **陨石撞击后的第二新重庆市没有停止运行。**

# 03｜Second New Chongqing is a bridge-network city

第二新重庆市不是建立在一整块连续“地面”上的城市。

It consists of many large engineered urban modules / districts:

- residential districts;
- public-service districts;
- hospitals;
- commercial areas;
- industrial and maintenance districts;
- transport hubs;
- energy / compute / life-support hubs;
- civic halls and public institutions.

Inside each district, ordinary residents still experience streets, floors, stations, buildings and plazas.

At city scale, however:

> **城区之间没有统一连续地面。**

They are separated by:

- structural cavities;
- maintenance voids;
- service decks;
- gravity-control layers;
- thermal layers;
- vacuum / pressure isolation structures;
- massive enclosed internal volumes.

Therefore the true city-scale circulation system is:

> **桥网，而不是地面道路网。**

# 04｜Chongqing spatial DNA

The project should not simply place Earth-Chongqing labels on generic science fiction.

The city inherits Chongqing's spatial logic:

> **山城多层关系 → 轨道巨城多层人工城区**
>
> **跨江桥梁 → 跨结构空腔的城市桥网**

There is no universal horizontal plane.

One district's floor may be another district's side / upper level / lower level when read at megastructure scale.

Normal artificial-gravity coordination makes this complexity feel ordinary before the disaster.

Post-impact fragmentation exposes the hidden geometry.

# 05｜Bridge hierarchy

The bridge network has four functional scales.

## Level 1 — city trunk bridge

Carries combinations of:

- heavy transit;
- rail;
- mass passenger movement;
- logistics;
- energy;
- communication;
- environmental circulation;
- gravity-reference handoff.

KV04 belongs to this family.

## Level 2 — regional bridge

Carries:

- commuters;
- smaller logistics;
- medical transfer;
- industrial robots;
- security / municipal operations.

## Level 3 — service / utility bridge

Carries:

- power;
- water;
- data;
- life support;
- gravity control linkage;
- other municipal services.

## Level 4 — maintenance catwalk / bridge

Carries:

- technicians;
- maintenance robots;
- local inspection operations.

Key rule:

> **桥不仅运人，也运城市状态。**

A bridge may carry people, power, data, environmental state, time synchronization and gravity-reference handoff at the same time.

Therefore bridge separation is not merely transport closure.

# 06｜Jialing River / 嘉陵江 — core series landmark

`LOCKED DIRECTION`

The project gains a major recurring megaspace called:

> **嘉陵江**

It is not a real river.

A possible dry engineering designation can exist internally, but residents and public culture call it **嘉陵江**.

It is a colossal enclosed structural canyon / longitudinal city cavity running through a major part of Second New Chongqing.

It contains:

- bridge layers at many elevations;
- rail bridges;
- utility bridges;
- maintenance platforms;
- gravity-control infrastructure;
- thermal / environmental systems;
- large municipal structures;
- deep but bounded engineered space.

Important spatial identity:

> **上面有桥，下面有桥，远处还有桥。**

The volume is monumental but still clearly artificial and finite.

---

# 07｜Why residents call it 嘉陵江

The name is not decorative localization.

It produces lived urban culture.

Residents naturally use expressions such as:

- 过江;
- 江北 / 江南;
- 上游 / 下游;
- 过江线;
- 江桥;
- 临江区域.

These terms no longer describe water hydrology literally.

They are inherited civic language adapted to an artificial orbital city.

This gives the city historical continuity without copying terrestrial Chongqing architecture one-to-one.

---

# 08｜Earth view inside Jialing River

嘉陵江 is mostly enclosed by:

- steel megastructure;
- service decks;
- pressure boundaries;
- bridge trusses;
- pipe groups;
- maintenance layers.

At a far structural end / observation or thermal aperture, the viewer may see:

> **遥远的地球。**

Earth must remain subordinate, not become a giant spectacle plate.

Preferred image:

> layers of bridges crossing a dark engineered canyon, civic lights at different depths, and a distant blue Earth visible through the far end of the structure.

This is a candidate signature establishing image for the series.

# 09｜Jialing River as a recurring dramatic space

嘉陵江 is not a one-off KV location.

It should recur at major stages of the season so the audience can measure city degradation physically.

Suggested progression:

### Early season

- many bridges active;
- dense but readable public circulation;
- city feels connected;
- full municipal life.

### Mid season

- some bridges closed;
- different banks / districts in different local day/night states;
- local gravity handoffs become unstable;
- fewer routes remain.

### Later season

- visible bridge loss;
- isolated districts degrade;
- temporary / low-capacity connections become lifelines;
- armed public systems appear at surviving interfaces.

### EP10

- evacuation and bridge separation occur in sequence;
- most crossings disappear;
- the cavity becomes visually more empty;
- the word “river” becomes emotionally literal because the two sides can no longer cross.

# 10｜Time fragmentation replaces winter / snow

`LOCKED DIRECTION`

Remove the series-level winter/snow mechanism.

The central temporal anomaly becomes:

> **不同城区拥有不同的本地现在。**

Possible simultaneous local states:

- morning;
- day;
- dusk;
- night;
- late night;
- pre-dawn.

This is not multiple suns or magical weather.

Second New Chongqing is largely an engineered internal environment.

Local “day / night” is expressed through:

- civic lighting cycles;
- public clocks;
- transport schedules;
- commercial rhythms;
- maintenance windows;
- hospital shift cycles;
- energy load management;
- artificial skylight / ceiling illumination;
- resident behavior.

No sky is required for the visual split.

---

# 11｜Time fragmentation is dramatic infrastructure

Different local times produce incompatible but locally rational public actions.

Example:

A district reads 07:30:

- morning peak transport;
- high passenger priority;
- full civic lighting;
- certain maintenance systems suppressed.

B district reads 23:10:

- night maintenance window;
- low transit capacity;
- different energy allocation;
- residential quiet cycle.

Both can be correct locally.

The shared bridge cannot simultaneously be both morning rush-hour trunk infrastructure and a night maintenance corridor.

Thus the series title becomes a real public-system question:

> **“重庆现在几点？”不再有唯一答案。**

Later the question becomes:

> **“谁的现在可以成为公共现在？”**

---

# 12｜Local gravity frames

`LOCKED: OPTION A`

The meteor / fragmentation damage does not create constant random gravity chaos.

Instead, districts stabilize themselves using local artificial-gravity references:

> **Local Gravity Frames**

Within a district, life is usually stable enough to continue.

The danger concentrates at connections.

A bridge must hand off between two districts whose local gravity vectors / reference solutions may no longer match.

Normal pre-disaster state:

> A gravity frame → controlled bridge transition → B gravity frame.

Post-impact state:

> A remains locally correct; B remains locally correct; the transition no longer has a reliable shared reference.

---

# 13｜Gravity must recur across the season

EP01's severe gravity inversion / fall is not a one-off spectacle.

Gravity anomaly has a severity ladder:

### Level 1

- hanging objects lean;
- loose tools drift / slide slightly;
- liquid surfaces reveal a small angle.

### Level 2

- people use rails;
- robots alter gait;
- carts / stretchers require restraint.

### Level 3

- cargo moves unexpectedly;
- medical transfer becomes difficult;
- a bridge must close or run only under narrow windows.

### Level 4

- local weight reduction / brief microgravity.

### Extreme

- severe directional inversion / fall hazard, as seen in EP01.

Rule:

> **Most episodes should use Level 1–3, not repeat full 180° inversion.**

This keeps gravity present as physical truth without turning every episode into a spectacle sequence.

---

# 14｜Jialing River is the best gravity showcase

Because the cavity is huge, the audience can see bridge systems with different orientation logic in the same frame.

Examples:

- one bridge reads visually horizontal;
- another distant bridge is oblique;
- another platform appears wall-like from the viewer's frame;
- people and robots still move normally inside their own local gravity solution.

Before the disaster, the city coordinates these frames invisibly.

After fragmentation, transitions become observable and dangerous.

This becomes a unique visual language of Second New Chongqing.

---

# 15｜Time and gravity remain independent failure axes

Do not make “different time” automatically cause “different gravity.”

They are separate results of shared-reference failure.

Therefore:

- two districts may share time but have gravity mismatch;
- two districts may have compatible gravity but different local time;
- some bridges may fail structurally while both time and gravity remain compatible;
- other interfaces may be physically intact but politically / temporally incompatible.

This allows episode variation.

# 16｜New definition of islanding / 群岛化

群岛化 becomes physically legible:

> **一个城区正在失去桥连接。**

Example progression:

8 usable bridges → 5 → 2 → 1.

Some remaining bridges may be degraded into:

- data-only;
- maintenance-only;
- one-way;
- low-capacity medical lifeline;
- temporary gravity-window operation.

Audience should understand isolation by seeing bridge density disappear, not only through dialogue.

# 17｜The Bridge (1969) influence — A + small B

User-approved influence level:

> **A：借核心结构与精神为主；少量 B：在动作 / 构图中有可感知致敬，但不翻拍。**

Do not copy:

The deeper dramatic principle to absorb is:

> **最难结束的不是已经坏掉的东西，而是仍然有价值、仍然能工作、甚至曾经救过人的连接。**

More specifically:

> **角色最终必须亲手结束自己此前一直在建立、维护、保护和依赖的连接。**

This must be built across the season, not introduced only in EP10.

# 18｜The final bridge must be familiar to the audience

The EP10 final bridge should not be a new anonymous super-bridge.

Recommended causal spine:

### EP03

A low-capacity connection across / through the Jialing River maintenance network is opened to save a patient / deliver medicine.

### EP04

Because the connection actually saves people, it remains in use and becomes normalized.

### Later episodes

It is reinforced, regulated and institutionalized into a real public lifeline.

### EP08

The same interface / bridge family becomes a place where two locally valid public-security orders physically overlap and armed systems face each other.

### EP10

This familiar lifeline survives longer than larger bridges because it remains useful for evacuation, maintenance and medical / essential transfer.

Eventually it becomes the final important connection that must be ended.

This gives the series a bridge with memory.

# 19｜The key Bridge-inspired reversal

Earlier in the series, characters fight to keep bridges alive.

EP10 must force them to separate a bridge that is still technically healthy.

Possible system state:

> STRUCTURE: NORMAL
>
> LOAD: NORMAL
>
> TRANSIT: AVAILABLE

The dramatic fact is:

> **桥没有坏。**

Yet the city must still close / separate it because the worlds on each side cannot remain inside one future common-state network.

This is a stronger expression of the Preservation Paradox than destroying already-failed infrastructure.

## Beat 1 — normal bridge confirmation

Male confirms the bridge remains physically viable.

The pause comes from the fact that his usual job is to identify what is broken; here, nothing is broken enough to make the choice easy.

## Beat 2 — final look before separation

The district is not exploding.

Possible continuing ordinary systems:

- public lights still on;
- empty station still functioning;

- one region still in its local day/dusk/night state.

Then the separation proceeds.

No heroic pose.

No copied shot.

# 21｜No explosion: bridge separation is procedural

The bridge finale should distinguish itself from war-film demolition.

Preferred physical action:

- traffic locks;
- rail power drops;
- data links exit;
- environmental lines isolate;
- gravity handoff shuts down;
- structural locks disengage;
- bridge segments slowly withdraw / separate.

Thus:

> **“断桥”是城市手术，不是爆破奇观。**

This maintains the project's procedural-horror / public-system tone while retaining the emotional structure of *The Bridge*.

# 22｜EP10 macro action becomes evacuation + bridge cutting

New finale macro action:

> **疏散 → 验证 → 断桥 → 区域退出**

At city scale:

1. evacuate districts that will not enter the future common Chongqing;
2. transfer people / material / required state;
3. verify bridge clearance;
4. close traffic functions;
5. close service/data/energy links;
6. exit gravity-transition functions;
7. mechanically separate bridge structures;
8. allow abandoned urban modules to leave the common city network.

This happens repeatedly across the city, not only once.

EP10 can therefore show a montage of bridge density decreasing.

# 23｜Final abandoned city fragment

A whole part of Second New Chongqing is ultimately abandoned.

It does not necessarily explode or vanish physically.

It becomes separated across four dimensions of shared cityhood:

- spatial / bridge connection;
- time reference;
- gravity reference;
- system / protocol reality.

Thus the city becomes physically smaller and politically / temporally more coherent.

# 24｜Private AI continuity and the bridge network

The private AI should not be treated as simply living in one mysterious server.

Her continuous operating subject has survived through the fragmented city's old compatibility environment:

- cross-district historical state;
- bridge-network data / dependencies;
- local-time divergence;
- local gravity mappings;
- old protocol compatibility layers;
- male's repeated movement across fragment boundaries.

Conceptually, without saying it in dialogue:

> **她自己也像一座看不见的桥。**

She carries continuity across realities that the city can no longer fully unify.

# 25｜Why preserving the AI has a real public cost

A technically feasible preservation route remains necessary.

If the city does **not** close the final old bridge / fragment condition:

- the same continuous private AI can survive;
- the old district / compatibility environment remains active;
- old time reference remains supported;
- old gravity reference remains supported;
- old protocol compatibility remains supported;
- the future bridge network must permanently preserve a failed fragmentation condition.

Therefore the cost is not abstract fairness.

It is:

> **为了两个人的连续关系，重庆必须永久维持一个不再属于共同未来的旧世界。**

The male must genuinely pursue this option before accepting its citywide cost.

The new series logic can be summarized as:

> **重庆本来依靠桥成为重庆。**
>
> **灾后，桥让每一块重庆都活了下来。**
