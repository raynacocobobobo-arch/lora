## Goal

Replace stale-occupancy/cutover EP01 with one coherent pilot:

> **A routine 302 gravity-reference maintenance task exposes an eight-minute split between Chongqing public time and 302 local time. The gravity instability window has already begun. The private AI eventually gains full gravity-control authority, but restoring nominal gravity immediately would kill the male operator, so she delays restoration and routes him toward a safe reset position on a small outer attached maintenance/interface segment. They genuinely begin completing Plan B; then a latent T0 interface/structural scar propagates under abnormal loading and that small attached segment loses its valid structural connection to 302. The AI again concludes that not restoring gravity is the least-bad option, abandons task completion for survival, later powers down to preserve life support, and an unidentified human hand catches the male. 302 itself remains.**

## Pilot opening function

EP01 establishes the world through an active maintenance task record, not lore lecture or delayed “they are in space” twist.

Opening must make legible through one ordinary commute/task intake:

1. setting = **第二新重庆市**, a finite modular ring-shipyard city;
2. T0 happened before EP01 and the city is in long-term recovery;
3. districts can run `LOCAL OPERATION` with Local Public Time and Local Gravity Frames;
4. common reference is degraded;
5. bridges/interfaces carry people plus time/gravity/protocol handoff;
6. male + private AI are already a mature pair;
7. today's task is routine: trusted handshake → reset 302 gravity reference → leave before safe window.

Spatial opening rule:

> **show ordinary district modules, multiple bridge scales and finite Jialing glimpses; do not require an infinite layered megacity or routine large-angle district orientation.**

## Episode dramatic architecture

### Cold Open — task record / ordinary city

Normal working day. Public transit, bridge hierarchy, local light/service differences, repairs and residents establish that the city lives.

Maintenance record narrows to 302 work order:

> T0 aftermath maintenance → 302 Local Operation → gravity-reference reset → public/local handshake required → safe reset window.

### Pressure 1 — routine task mostly works

Early nodes clear. Only final trusted handshake fails. Small gravity-drift evidence appears but fits the known job.

Why male must be there:

> remote systems cannot create the trusted current local/public physical bridge; he must access the old relay inside the same 302 district while AI performs protocol/model work.

Spatial lock:

> **302 is an outer legacy district cluster, one side city-facing and one side service/space-facing. The male does not descend through multiple city districts.**

### Pressure 2 — partial handshake reveals the real clock

Partial handshake restores trusted local safety/time channel, not gravity control.

Trusted comparison:

- `CHONGQING PUBLIC`
- `302 LOCAL`
- `+08:00`

Safe reset window already expired; known gravity re-lock/instability cycle already active.

Time does **not** cause gravity failure. The stale shared time mapping caused the crew to misjudge where the known gravity cycle was.

### Dominant Turn — full control arrives too late for the original action

During evacuation, AI continues the handshake over the physical bridge already established.

`FULL HANDSHAKE` succeeds; nominal restore is technically available.

Male asks to restore. AI refuses because male/debris/loads have redistributed under abnormal gravity; immediate nominal restore would reverse those loads and likely kill him.

Single dominant turn:

> **goal changes from “restore gravity now” to “delay restoration until he reaches a safe reset position.”**

Plan B still intends to restore nominal gravity and finish the original task.

### Payoff — exploit bad state to reach safe reset

Safe reset position is located on:

> **a small outer attached maintenance/interface segment connected to 302.**

The abnormal vector becomes the route.

Male reaches the point and is genuinely secured; recovery begins; AI prepares `RESTORE`.

### Cost after solution — the attached segment loses connection

Not:

- AI error;
- male miss;
- random tether break;
- deliberate occupied-module ejection.

Locked function:

> **a latent T0 scar in the attachment/load path propagates under prolonged abnormal-direction loading. After recovery has genuinely begun, the small attached maintenance/interface segment itself loses valid structural connection to 302 and accidentally detaches.**

Therefore:

> plan valid → recovery works → physical fact changes → Plan B can no longer finish.

302 remains attached to the ring city.

AI reassesses and still does not restore nominal gravity because restoration cannot safely return the male and still creates lethal reverse loads.

This is Cost, not a second dominant turn.

### End progression — task gives way to survival

Male + detached small segment cross beyond effective Local Gravity Frame; artificial acceleration ends; inertia carries them outward.

External city view follows Spatial V2:

- readable curved ring-city mass;
- district-module clusters;
- different bridge scales;
- finite Jialing urban corridor relationship;
- no infinite layered hull / endless bridge abyss.

AI uses EVA capability only to reduce rotation/outbound velocity. These actions work but cannot return him.

Story decelerates. No device cascade.

As suit power becomes critical, AI shuts high-compute capabilities first, then local core to maximize life support + rescue beacon.

Approved final direction:

> “我很抱歉。”
>
> “祝你早安……晚安。”

No dialogue after shutdown.

An unidentified EVA-gloved human hand catches his wrist; second hand reaches the rescue ring/tether. EP02 reveals the rescuer.
