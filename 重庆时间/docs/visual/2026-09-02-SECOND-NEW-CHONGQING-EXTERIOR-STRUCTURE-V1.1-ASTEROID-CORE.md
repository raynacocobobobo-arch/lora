# 第二新重庆外部结构设定 V1.1 — Asteroid Core

- Updated: 2026-09-02
- Project: **《重庆时间》**
- City: **第二新重庆市**
- Status: `ACTIVE NON-CANON VISUAL / ENGINEERING EXECUTION SPEC`
- Canon basis: `SPATIAL-ENVIRONMENT-CANON-PATCH-V2.1-ASTEROID-CORE.md` + `SPATIAL-ENVIRONMENT-CANON-V2.md`
- Supersedes for exterior execution: `2026-09-02-SECOND-NEW-CHONGQING-EXTERIOR-STRUCTURE-V1.md`
- Purpose: visualize the asteroid-centered ring-city with physically credible structure, material layering, T0 impact repair history, and the approved cel-animation language.

---

# 00｜Exterior identity

Second New Chongqing must read from the outside as:

> **engineered central asteroid core + permanent ring-shipyard backbone + district modules + radial core connections + multi-scale bridge/service network.**

The city is not hollow.

The asteroid must be visible as a real central mass integrated into the city.

The city is also not a uniform solid donut.

---

# 01｜Central asteroid visual hierarchy

The asteroid is the primary central mass.

It should show:

- irregular natural rock silhouette;
- visible geological surface in selected areas;
- engineered reinforcement / containment lattice;
- central structural collar / dock frame;
- deep anchor zones or excavated attachment corridors where visually useful;
- service structures partially wrapping the asteroid;
- several major radial structural / service trusses extending outward toward the ring.

Do not cover the entire asteroid with a smooth metal shell.

The contrast between natural rock mass and engineered structure is part of the visual identity.

---

# 02｜Asteroid physical credibility

Do not depict the asteroid as loose regolith magically carrying the city.

Visualize load transfer through:

- deep anchor hardware;
- reinforced load-transfer zones;
- external lattice / restraint structures;
- structural collar nodes;
- redundant radial truss connections;
- inspection / maintenance access;
- isolation / displacement-compensation joints at selected transitions.

The asteroid may support structural / industrial functions, but its natural gravity is not the city's normal 1G system.

---

# 03｜Permanent Ring Backbone

The permanent ring surrounds the asteroid at larger radius.

Visual language:

- segmented box girders;
- large circumferential space trusses;
- major node frames;
- district attachment collars;
- secondary braces;
- service corridors;
- repair access;
- occasional radial structural relationships to the asteroid-core dock.

The ring must not look like one continuous thick armor tube.

---

# 04｜Radial core connections

The central asteroid and ring are visually tied by a limited number of major radial structures.

Possible functions:

- structural support / alignment;
- cargo / logistics;
- utilities;
- inspection;
- industrial service;
- docking / maintenance access.

They are NOT automatically public bridges.

They should be visibly different from:

- Jialing Trunk Bridge;
- regional bridges;
- Familiar Lifeline.

Exact count remains TBD; use enough to make the macro structure credible without turning the image into a bicycle wheel.

---

# 05｜Bounded central work volumes

There are still open volumes around the asteroid, but the city should not read as a hollow donut.

Central space may include:

- bounded docking corridors;
- maintenance craft routes;
- cargo transfer volumes;
- inspection clearances;
- industrial handling structures;
- structural access zones.

Correct reading:

> **asteroid-filled core with engineered working clearances around it.**

Incorrect reading:

> **giant empty black hole in the middle of a torus.**

---

# 06｜District Modules

District modules remain distributed around the ring.

They should vary by:

- construction generation;
- size;
- exterior panel family;
- repair history;
- docking collar generation;
- paint / numbering age;
- attached service equipment.

Ordinary lived-city modules should not be replaced by asteroid tunnels.

---

# 07｜Jialing exterior relationship

Jialing remains a public urban corridor inside the ring-city geography.

From selected exterior/oblique views, it may be possible to visually infer a band/open corridor on the inner urban side and frame the central asteroid beyond it.

But never label or draw:

- asteroid = Jialing;
- central gap = Jialing;
- a natural river cutting across the asteroid.

---

# 08｜302 exterior relationship

302 remains an outer legacy district cluster.

Exterior orientation must still support:

> **one side faces city / ring interior; one side faces space.**

302 should appear as an older, more patched outer module family, but it is not the central asteroid core.

---

# 09｜Materials

Execution assumptions for plausible visualization:

## Primary ring / heavy nodes

- high-strength steel-like and titanium-alloy-like high-load nodes;
- aluminum / aluminum-lithium-like large lightweight structural shell/frame language;
- composite reinforcement on selected long spans;
- deep bolted / clamped / welded serviceable node architecture rather than seamless magic joints.

## Asteroid restraint

- heavy anchor collars;
- drilled/deep anchor structures;
- high-strength restraint trusses;
- sacrificial / replaceable interface elements;
- inspection ports and strain-monitoring hardware;
- shock / vibration isolation where required.

## Habitation protection

- pressure shell;
- insulation;
- radiation protection;
- micrometeoroid/debris protection;
- replaceable outer panels;
- Whipple-shield-like layered protection language where appropriate.

## Thermal management

- thin heat-radiator surfaces;
- protected fluid routing;
- limited radiator farms in service areas;
- do not use thick decorative radiator armor.

---

# 10｜T0 meteor impact + maintenance scars

The central asteroid is NOT the T0 impactor.

T0 impact damage appears elsewhere on the ring/district/interface system.

Required chronology:

> **impact → emergency sealing → structural reinforcement → long-term maintenance**

Required visual cues:

- irregular torn impact zone;
- damaged sacrificial shielding;
- peeled or fractured outer panels;
- exposed structural members;
- local burn / ablation marks;
- emergency closure panels;
- later reinforcement frame;
- mismatched replacement plates;
- old repair patches that have themselves weathered;
- inspection ports and maintenance routing;
- repeated service history.

Overall city state:

> **severely wounded in the past, continuously maintained, still inhabited and operational.**

---

# 11｜Approved art-style inheritance

Use the existing approved visual DNA:

- serious cinematic cel-animation background art;
- hand-drawn architectural linework;
- 2–3 hard-edged cel-shading tiers;
- large shapes first, micro-detail second;
- simplified monumental industrial forms;
- restrained signage;
- low glossy reflection;
- low micro-greeble density;
- aged off-white + cold gray-blue + deep steel blue;
- restrained faded industrial red / safety yellow;
- Chinese civic-industrial retro-futurist identity.

Do not turn the asteroid surface into photoreal VFX while the city remains cel-shaded. Rock and engineering structures must belong to the same drawn visual system.

# 12｜Full generation prompt

```text
第二新重庆市外部结构总览，SECOND NEW CHONGQING，2.35:1 超宽电影构图，成熟动画电影级大型赛璐璐环境设定图。

第二新重庆不是中空的甜甜圈空间站，而是一座以一颗被捕获、稳定化并工程化固定的小行星为中央核心，在其外围建设永久环形船坞骨架、城区模块、多尺度桥网和服务连接的模块化中国轨道城市。

主环中心线半径视觉量级约 1 km，主要城市内部最大有效净空约 200 m。环形只定义城市拓扑和永久承力骨架，绝对不要使用 Stanford torus、旋转产生1G、向心重力或小行星天然重力提供全城1G的视觉解释。

画面中央必须明确存在一颗真实的小行星核心。小行星保持明显天然不规则岩体轮廓，局部可见深色硅酸盐/碳质岩面、金属富集暴露区、裂隙和被工程稳定后的表面；不要把整颗小行星包成光滑金属球。

小行星已经被城市工程化：在关键区域有深部锚固结构、贯穿式或钻孔式固定点、外部高强度约束桁架、中央结构 collar / dock frame、检查节点和维护平台。多个有限数量的大型径向结构/服务桁架从中央小行星核心向外围永久主环延伸，使观众一眼理解这座环城是围绕中央小行星构建并与其建立工程关系的，而不是围着空洞建造。

径向连接不要做成密集自行车辐条；保持少量、粗壮、功能明确、具有冗余结构和接口节点。它们承担结构、物流、工业服务、维护和部分公共工程功能，但不要全部画成普通公共桥。

小行星和主环之间仍保留有边界的作业/运输空间，可有维护艇、货运路径、对接平台、工程机械臂和结构检修空域，但中央绝对不能读成巨大的空洞。

外围永久主环不是一整块厚重的金属甜甜圈外壳，而是由分段箱梁、空间桁架、环向主承力梁、大型节点框架、冗余载荷路径和标准化城区接口构成的永久环形船坞骨架。

大量不同年代、不同尺寸的城区模块沿主环分段挂接。它们之间存在标准化连接节点、位移/热胀补偿、隔振结构、气密接口和维护空间。城区模块必须能读成真正容纳住宅、商业、医院、车站、学校和公共生活的大型城市舱段，而不是纯机械舱。

城市不是崭新的：不同模块有不同代际外板、褪色程度、维修补丁、编号、接口版本和外接工程设备。主体颜色 aged off-white / dirty ivory / cold gray-blue / deep steel blue，少量 faded industrial red 和 muted safety yellow。

外侧布置工业、EVA、维护、热管理、对接和服务模块。散热器使用薄型工程热辐射面，不做厚重装甲片。外壳体现压力壳、隔热、辐射/微流星防护和可替换外板的分层结构，可参考 Whipple-shield-like layered protection language。

材料结构视觉可信：高载节点采用钛合金/高强钢类结构语言，大型轻质压力舱和主壳采用铝合金/铝锂合金类结构语言，局部长跨构件使用碳纤维复合材料加强。小行星锚固区使用重型高强度节点、约束桁架、可检查连接、隔振/位移补偿，不允许裸岩直接魔法承重。

画面同时必须出现 T0 历史陨石撞击和长期维护痕迹，但中央小行星不是 T0 撞击体。T0 撞击痕迹位于外围某个城区/主环/接口区域，形成不规则撕裂式撞击区，外层防护破坏、外板翻卷、内部桁架暴露、局部烧蚀黑化；同时能读出 impact → emergency sealing → structural reinforcement → long-term maintenance 四个阶段。加入后装加强框、异色替换板、老化的应急封堵、维修补丁、维护管线、检测节点和长期修复历史。

整体感觉：这是一座以中央小行星为核心逐步建设出来、经历长期扩建与维修、遭受过严重撞击但从未停止运行的中国轨道城市。

视觉风格严格继承已批准《重庆时间》赛璐璐环境语言：serious cinematic cel-animation background art, hand-drawn architectural linework, 2–3 hard-edged cel-shading tiers, large shapes first, micro-detail second, simplified monumental industrial forms, clear silhouettes, restrained signage, low glossy reflection, low micro-greeble density, mature Chinese retro-futurist civic-industrial design。

小行星岩体也必须以同一套手绘赛璐璐方式表现，不要突然变成照片级CG岩石。

远处地球/星空只能作为轻微空间参照，不得压过第二新重庆本身。
```
