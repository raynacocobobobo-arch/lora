# 00｜Core definition

**Chinese title:**

> 《第二新重庆｜外部结构总览》

**English title:**

> `SECOND NEW CHONGQING — EXTERIOR STRUCTURE OVERVIEW`

Core definition:

> **第二新重庆是一座建立在永久环形船坞骨架上的模块化轨道城市。**

It is not a monolithic cast “donut spacecraft”. It is a long-lived ring-city structural system assembled through decades of construction, district-module attachment, replacement, impact repair, reinforcement and continuing maintenance.

Canonical scale direction:

- main-ring centerline radius: approximately `1 km`;
- visual overall diameter order: approximately `2 km`;
- maximum effective interior urban clear-height: approximately `200 m`.

These are visual / dramatic scale directions, not construction tolerances.

Hard correction:

> **The ring defines topology and structural organization. It does NOT generate artificial gravity through rotation.**

Never restore the discarded `22 km diameter`, `centrifugal 1G`, `inward gravity`, or Stanford-torus reading.

---

# 01｜Permanent Ring Backbone

## PRIMARY RING STRUCTURE / PERMANENT

The permanent ring backbone is the oldest and most persistent structural system of the city.

Primary responsibilities:

- maintain global geometry and structural continuity;
- carry large district-module attachment loads;
- provide redundant load paths;
- provide large standardized interfaces and transport nodes;
- support long-term module replacement, expansion and repair;
- survive local module retirement without requiring the ring itself to be cut.

Visual rule:

> **Read the ring as a city skeleton / shipyard backbone, not as one giant pressurized rotating habitat.**

Preferred structural language:

- segmented box-girder systems;
- large space-truss members;
- circumferential load-bearing beams;
- deep node frames around major module interfaces;
- redundant secondary braces;
- visible expansion / displacement compensation zones at selected interfaces;
- external inspection and maintenance routes.

The outer cladding must never imply that the entire ring is one homogeneous thick armor shell.

---

# 02｜District Modules

## URBAN DISTRICT MODULES

Large lived-city modules attached to the ring backbone.

A district module can contain ordinary city functions:

- streets;
- housing;
- schools;
- hospitals;
- retail;
- station / transit spaces;
- public halls;
- cinema;
- plazas;
- industrial / municipal service spaces.

Audience reading rule:

> **Residents live in districts, not in equipment compartments.**

The city has been expanded and repaired over long periods, so district modules should not all look identical.

Allowed variation:

- different construction generations;
- different panel scales;
- different interface revisions;
- repainting and faded coating;
- new replacement shells beside older shells;
- different maintenance patches;
- revised docking collars and service attachments;
- numbering / municipal markings that show history rather than perfect manufacturing uniformity.

---

# 03｜Inner Urban Belt

## INNER URBAN BELT

The ring-facing urban side that concentrates lived-city and public-transport functions.

May contain:

- ordinary residential / commercial districts;
- transit;
- public-service facilities;
- bridgeheads;
- district interfaces;
- Jialing-facing neighborhoods;
- public corridors and civic activity.

Important Jialing correction:

> **嘉陵江 is NOT the central torus hole.**

It is a finite, continuous, hundred-meter-scale open public urban corridor **inside the ring-city geography**, with lived districts and multiple bridge scales around it.

Do not place a giant “Jialing city island” or garden disk in the ring center.

---

# 04｜Outer Urban / Service Belt

## OUTER URBAN BELT — SERVICE / INDUSTRY

The more engineering-exposed outer side of the city.

Functions may include:

- industrial service areas;
- EVA support;
- maintenance;
- thermal management;
- gravity-service interfaces;
- docking;
- external inspection;
- logistics;
- structural support and module-change work.

Visual difference from the lived inner side:

> Outer areas may expose more structure, maintenance rails, service trusses, heat-rejection systems, docking collars and interface hardware.

---

# 05｜Attached Service / Maintenance Modules

## AUXILIARY MAINTENANCE / SERVICE MODULES

Physically much smaller than district modules.

Typical functions:

- maintenance platform;
- EVA interface;
- equipment compartment;
- gravity-service node;
- external inspection unit;
- local power / environmental unit;
- isolatable interface segment;
- small attached structural service section.

This is the canonical third physical scale.

EP01 spatial consequence:

> The accidentally detached structure is this class of **small attached outer maintenance/interface segment**, not the whole 302 district.

---

# 06｜Docking and Transport Interfaces

## DOCKING & TRANSPORT INTERFACES

Protruding external engineering interfaces distributed around the ring.

Possible functions:

- personnel transfer;
- cargo transfer;
- maintenance craft docking;
- engineering logistics;
- district-module installation / replacement;
- external inspection and repair.

Visual rule:

> These nodes may look more mechanical, open and load-bearing than ordinary district shells.

---

# 07｜Primary Docking / Transport Hub

## PRIMARY DOCKING & TRANSPORT HUB

A limited number of larger nodes can exist for major logistics and module handling.

They must remain:

- local attachments to the ring-city structure;
- visibly larger than ordinary service interfaces;
- smaller in conceptual importance than the entire city;
- not a new central megastation floating in the ring void.

---

# 08｜Central shipyard / structural void

The ring center remains primarily:

> **central shipyard / structural open volume**

Allowed elements:

- sparse long-span transport connections;
- limited docking / maintenance structures;
- structural cross-links where functionally needed;
- small engineering craft;
- inspection or cargo movement.

Forbidden:

- giant central floating city;
- giant central circular garden;
- central continent-like urban disk;
- “Jialing River = torus center hole”;
- giant artificial lake / ground city suspended in the center;
- mandatory central reactor spectacle.

The open center exists as part of the overall shipyard geometry, not as the main lived-city image.

---

# 09｜Structural section logic

Recommended visual layering from inner/lived side toward external service side:

> **inner urban/public belt → district-module structural layer → permanent ring backbone → outer service/industrial layer → docking / EVA / maintenance attachments**

This is a design-reading hierarchy, not a mandatory continuous sandwich cross-section everywhere.

## Mass / balance structures

Local mass-balancing or counterweight-like structures may exist for:

- center-of-mass management;
- attitude-control support;
- structural load balancing after module attachment / retirement;
- local vibration control;
- temporary module handling.

But they must never be labeled or visualized as the source of 1G artificial gravity.

---

# 10｜Materials and structural language

## 10.1 Canon vs execution assumption

Canon locks the city as modular, maintainable, repeatedly repaired, and structurally connected through a permanent ring backbone.

The specific materials below are **engineering-visualization assumptions**, not new hard Canon unless separately promoted.

## 10.2 Primary structural members

Preferred visual logic:

- high-strength metallic primary frames;
- deep joints at major module interfaces;
- light but stiff large-span members;
- modular replaceable secondary structures;
- external inspection access.

Suggested real-world-derived material language for visualization:

- titanium-alloy / high-strength steel-like high-load nodes;
- aluminum / aluminum-lithium-like lightweight pressure-hull and large frame language;
- carbon-fiber-composite-like selected long-span reinforcement;
- flexible metallic/composite interface structures where displacement accommodation is required.

Do not imply that every visible plate is a primary load-bearing armor member.

## 10.3 Protective shell hierarchy

Exterior shell should visually imply layered protection rather than one huge armor slab.

Recommended language:

1. pressure hull / airtight structural shell;
2. thermal insulation layer;
3. radiation-protection zones where appropriate;
4. micrometeoroid / orbital-debris protection;
5. replaceable exterior panels / sacrificial outer surfaces.

`Whipple-shield-like` layered protection is an acceptable visual reference.

## 10.4 Flexible seals / interface compensation

At module interfaces, include plausible provisions for:

- displacement tolerance;
- thermal expansion;
- vibration isolation;
- service-line decoupling;
- pressure sealing;
- inspection and replacement.

These should read as engineered joints, not magical seamless welds.

## 10.5 Thermal management

External radiators / heat-rejection surfaces may appear.

Visual rule:

> Radiators are thin engineered heat-rejection surfaces, not thick armor plates.

Use limited, functionally placed radiator fields rather than covering the entire ring with decorative fins.

## 10.6 Maintenance access

Visible external maintenance language may include:

- rails;
- access tracks;
- inspection hatches;
- EVA anchor points;
- robotic-arm bases;
- removable service panels;
- cable / pipe runs in protected channels;
- small service platforms.

# 11｜Meteor impact history

The exterior design must visibly carry the history of T0.

## METEOR IMPACT SCAR — HISTORICAL

The city is not shown at the instant of impact.

The visual must communicate the sequence:

> **impact → emergency initial sealing → structural reinforcement → long-term maintenance**

The exterior therefore contains several different time layers simultaneously:

1. original damaged surface;
2. emergency sealing / temporary closure;
3. later structural reinforcement;
4. long-term replacement and recurring maintenance.

The city should read as:

> **badly wounded in the past, continuously maintained, still working.**

Not:

> freshly destroyed / abandoned / post-apocalyptic wreck.

---

# 12｜Impact-zone visual anatomy

Do NOT use a clean circular “bullet hole”.

Preferred damage / repair cues:

- irregular tearing impact cavity;
- fragmented / peeled outer protection layers;
- exposed internal frame or truss;
- local ablation / burn-blackening;
- deformed sacrificial shielding;
- emergency closure panels;
- later reinforcement frame;
- replacement plates with mismatched color / age;
- weld / bolt / clamp repair language;
- temporary braces that became semi-permanent;
- new monitoring ports;
- exposed maintenance routing;
- repaint boundaries and patch history.

Repair chronology should be legible:

- some parts visibly old and scorched;
- some emergency repair pieces aged afterward;
- some reinforcement pieces newer than surrounding shell;
- inspection and maintenance continued after the initial repair.

---

# 13｜External visual identity

Second New Chongqing should NOT read as:

- a pristine luxury spacecraft;
- a generic NASA concept station;
- a Star-Wars-like battleship;
- a glossy cyberpunk mothership;
- an all-new white Apple-like orbital habitat.

It should read as:

> **a long-lived Chinese orbital city that has been expanded, patched, repaired, serviced and inhabited for decades.**

Primary palette:

- aged off-white / dirty ivory;
- cold gray-blue;
- deep steel blue;
- dark iron gray.

Secondary accents:

- faded industrial red;
- muted safety yellow;
- limited municipal blue signage;
- sparse warm practical lighting.

No decorative neon overload.

---

# 14｜Cel-animation rendering lock

The exterior must inherit the approved 《重庆时间》 large-environment art system.

Primary rendering direction:

- serious cinematic cel-animation background art;
- hand-drawn architectural linework;
- 2–3 hard-edged cel-shading tiers;
- large structural silhouettes first;
- micro-detail second;
- simplified monumental industrial forms;
- restrained signage;
- low glossy reflection noise;
- low micro-greeble density;
- cold restrained palette;
- damage / repair history expressed through large readable patches and structural changes rather than phototexture clutter.

Primary style lesson inherited from KV05:

> **large shapes first; micro-detail second.**

Do not turn this into photoreal 3D hard-surface concept art.

# 17｜Full generation prompt — RING-ORIENT-01 / CITY-03

```text
第二新重庆市外部结构总览，SECOND NEW CHONGQING，2.35:1 超宽电影构图。

一座建立在永久环形船坞骨架上的模块化中国轨道城市，整体是不规则但工程上可信的环形城市结构，主环中心线半径视觉量级约 1 km，整体直径量级约 2 km，主要城市内部最大有效净空约 200 m。环形只定义城市拓扑，绝对不要表现为旋转产生人工重力的 Stanford torus，不要任何“向心重力”“旋转1G”箭头或视觉暗示。

整体结构必须符合物理学和材料学逻辑：

永久主环不是一整块巨厚甜甜圈外壳，而是一套由分段箱梁、空间桁架、环向承力梁、大型节点框架和冗余载荷路径组成的永久船坞/承力骨架。

大量不同年代、不同尺寸的城区模块沿主环的内侧和外侧分段挂接。模块之间存在明确的标准化连接节点、伸缩位移补偿段、隔振接口、柔性气密结构和维修空间。

城区模块应该读成真正能够容纳住宅、商业、医院、车站和公共城市生活的大型舱段，而不是纯机械设备舱。

环内侧为主要城市生活面和公共交通面；嘉陵江是位于环形城市内部城区体系中的有限、连续、百米级开放公共城市带，绝对不要把嘉陵江画成中央圆孔或中央悬浮城市。

环中央大部分保持为空旷的中央船坞 / 结构空域，只允许少量大型运输连接、维护桁架、对接结构和小型工程飞行器穿过，不允许中央出现悬浮圆盘城市、中央花园世界或巨大核心岛。

环外侧布置工业、服务、EVA、热管理、维修、重力服务和对接模块。存在少量明显向外突出的 docking and transport interfaces、maintenance service modules、engineering platforms。

主承力体系强调高强度金属/复合材料混合结构：
高载荷节点使用钛合金、高强钢类结构语言；
大型轻质压力舱和外壳表现铝合金/铝锂合金结构；
局部长跨构件可见碳纤维复合材料加强；
外壳由压力壳、隔热层、辐射防护和微流星防护系统组成；
使用 Whipple-shield-like layered protection language，而不是一整块巨厚装甲。

在外侧结构布置合理的薄型散热器和热管理结构，散热器必须是轻薄展开工程面，不是厚装甲板。

城市经过几十年长期扩建、更新、维修，因此不同模块不能完全一致：
有不同年代面板，不同版本接口，不同程度褪色，不同维修补丁，不同编号体系。
主体为 aged off-white / dirty ivory / cold gray-blue / deep steel blue，少量 faded industrial red 和 muted safety yellow。

画面必须有明显的历史陨石撞击区。

撞击位置位于一个城区/环外壳交界区域，形成不规则撕裂式撞击坑，局部多层防护壳破损、外板翻卷、内部桁架暴露，有烧蚀和黑化痕迹，但不是正在爆炸。

撞击已经过去很久，必须同时读出四阶段：
impact → emergency initial sealing → structural reinforcement → long-term maintenance。

在撞击区附近增加后装加强框、异色替换外板、维修补丁、附加桁架、临时焊接件、维护管线、检测接口和长期维修痕迹。
部分维修件明显比原始结构更新，部分应急修补已经再次老化。

让观众感觉：
这座城市曾遭受严重撞击，但没有停止运行；
人们不是把它修回崭新，而是不断让受损的城市继续工作。

画面中可以有少量外部维护艇、维修机械臂、EVA工程平台、小型工程飞行器作为尺度参照，但不要形成太空舰队。

远处可以极少量出现地球弧面或太空环境，只作为尺度参照，不得压过第二新重庆本身。

视觉风格完全继承已批准的《重庆时间》大型赛璐璐环境风格：
serious cinematic cel-animation background art，
hand-drawn architectural linework，
2–3 hard-edged cel-shading tiers，
large shapes first, micro-detail second，
simplified monumental industrial forms，
clear structural silhouettes，
restrained signage，
low glossy reflection，
low micro-greeble density，
mature Chinese retro-futurist civic-industrial design。

画面不是 NASA 科普写实图，不是 photoreal 3D render，不是 dense hard-surface kitbash，不是 cyberpunk neon city，不是 Star Wars 战舰。

整体应该像一部成熟动画电影中使用的“第二新重庆城市外部总设定图”：
结构物理可信，
材料层级可信，
维修历史可信，
城市生活规模可信，
视觉上冷静、克制、巨大、受损但仍在工作。
```
