# 03｜What “Dungeon Style” means in this project

“Dungeon Style” is an internal production label for a highly graphic, simplified, design-forward sci-fi illustration language.

It is defined by the following combination:

> **strong black/white negative-space design + large off-white industrial masses + sparse rust-red functional accents + controlled ink-like linework + hard cel shadow blocks + visible age/repair history + low micro-detail density.**

It is not simply “black-and-white manga”.

It must still preserve:

- credible space/function;
- readable civic infrastructure;
- mature industrial weight;
- lived-city history;
- Seedance-friendly stable forms.

---

# 04｜Core graphic hierarchy

Readability order:

1. **major silhouette / spatial relationship**;
2. **large black-white shape distribution**;
3. **one dominant functional/dramatic state**;
4. **large structural joints / circulation / equipment**;
5. **rust-red / red-white graphic accents**;
6. **signage / slogans**;
7. **maintenance / damage marks**;
8. **micro detail last**.

If micro-detail competes with silhouette, simplify.

If a frame starts looking like premium hard-surface 3D concept art, simplify.

If there are too many tiny lights/panel seams/pipes for Seedance to hold consistently, simplify.

---

# 05｜Black / white / gray composition

Primary visual identity:

- deep black negative space or deep charcoal shadow masses;
- large off-white / ivory architectural surfaces;
- mid-gray structural transitions;
- charcoal / black structural voids;
- very sparse warm practical light.

The black areas are not “missing detail”.

They are active graphic composition.

Use black to:

- separate large structures;
- create depth;
- preserve clear silhouettes;
- suppress unnecessary environment noise;
- create designed negative space.

Do not fill every black area with pipes, lights, fog or stars.

---

# 06｜Rust-red / red-white industrial motif

This is now a key Dungeon Style design motif.

Preferred accent:

> **muted rust red / faded industrial red**

Use it on a limited number of:

- module bands;
- repair sectors;
- old structural zones;
- standardized function markers;
- maintenance shutters;
- civic text/signage;
- selected robot/equipment surfaces.

Red-white alternating structural bands are encouraged where functionally plausible.

The red-white pattern should feel like:

> **industrial module identification / civic engineering paint system**

not:

> decorative candy stripes or racing graphics.

Red must remain subordinate to the black-white architecture.

Recommended coverage:

> typically about **5–20% of the main visible structure**, depending on scene function.

Do not make every object red-white.

---

# 07｜Linework and surface design

Linework:

- visible;
- confident;
- slightly irregular / illustrated rather than CAD-perfect;
- used to describe large panel breaks and joints;
- restrained on broad surfaces.

Surface detail:

- large panel grouping;
- selected seams;
- selected access hatches;
- a few exposed braces;
- repair plates;
- structural break lines;
- limited bolts/weld cues.

Avoid:

- every-surface panelization;
- dense greeble;
- hundreds of tiny antennae;
- tiny technical lettering everywhere;
- photoreal texture noise.

---

# 08｜Cel-shading / material rule

Lighting is primarily graphic.

Use:

- 2–3 major hard-edged shade tiers;
- large cast-shadow masses;
- restrained gradients only when needed;
- low specular noise;
- very limited bloom;
- matte / aged painted industrial surfaces.

Materials should read through shape and wear hierarchy rather than physically based reflection detail.

Preferred material family:

- aged off-white painted structural panels;
- dark steel / composite framing;
- charcoal rock/concrete-like masses where relevant;
- muted rust-red industrial paint;
- black rubber/seal/joint material;
- repair plates / exposed reinforcement.

---

# 09｜Age, meteor impact and maintenance history

Second New Chongqing is not pristine.

The Dungeon Style lane preserves the established principle:

> **maintained / used / repaired / still working.**

Damage language should be simplified but unmistakable.

For T0-related exterior assets, use large readable cues:

- one clear torn/removed shell region;
- exposed deeper frame;
- a few strong reinforcement members;
- dark ablation/burn mass;
- mismatched replacement plates;
- rust-red repair band / legacy structural zone;
- temporary-looking repair that has itself aged.

Do not scatter hundreds of tiny cracks across the whole image.

The city should look wounded, not visually noisy.

---

# 10｜Signage and slogan system

Dungeon Style allows stronger graphic text than the earlier purely restrained municipal-signage rule, but text remains part of the architecture, not an explanatory overlay.

## Allowed

- large civic/municipal identifiers integrated into wall/module surfaces;
- one or two large slogans when composition needs them;
- large zone numbers;
- simple facility/route identifiers.

## Current approved exterior text behavior

The master image establishes this distinction:

> **“第二新重庆市” may appear as a large in-world municipal identity painted/printed on the structure.**

This is NOT the same as placing a floating movie title at the top of the frame.

Also valid as an in-world civic slogan in the approved master:

> **“科学引领未来”**

Future slogan content is scene-dependent and must not contradict story tone.

## Forbidden / avoid

- floating cover-title text unless explicitly requested;
- explanatory infographics;
- technical paragraphs;
- many tiny labels;
- multiple competing slogans;
- text that explains the plot instead of the environment.

Rule:

> **text participates in composition; it does not carry the scene logic.**

---

# 11｜Seedance-friendly design requirements

Every setting image intended for motion generation should optimize for temporal stability from the start.

Prioritize:

- strong stable silhouette;
- large stable surfaces;
- low count of dominant structural members;
- consistent red-white bands;
- one readable damage/repair landmark;
- broad black negative spaces;
- simplified distant background.

Suppress:

- dense windows;
- dense stars;
- tiny pipes;
- micro-grids;
- dense scaffolding;
- repeated tiny lamps;
- tiny surface text;
- overly fine rock texture;
- hundreds of similar mechanical parts.

Production hierarchy:

> **motion stability > macro structure > graphic identity > material continuity > micro detail.**

---

# 12｜Composition rules

Default aspect ratio for major cinematic setting images:

> **2.35:1**

Preferred composition:

- one main spatial relationship;
- one dominant state;
- strong negative-space design;
- 3–5 large masses rather than dozens of competing small masses;
- off-center composition allowed and encouraged when it improves design;
- structure should remain readable at thumbnail size.

Do not force every scene into the same exterior-ring composition.

The style is reusable; the composition belongs to the script location and dramatic action.

---

# 13｜Character / robot / object extension rule

Dungeon Style can extend to robots, vehicles and props, but the design must remain functional.

For equipment:

- simple major silhouette;
- large off-white/black massing;
- optional limited rust-red / red-white zone marking;
- clear joints/tools;
- low ornamental armor;
- wear/repair history.

For characters:

> this document does not automatically replace any existing character-specific or Oshii-style character rendering lane.

Character style routing must follow explicit user direction / active character asset lock.

# 17｜Anti-drift summary

Dungeon Style is NOT:

- generic black-and-white manga;
- photoreal hard-SF with a toon filter;
- cyberpunk;
- luxury sci-fi;
- dense machinery wallpaper;
- a mandate to put red stripes everywhere;
- a mandate to put slogans in every frame;
- a replacement for story/spatial Canon;
- a replacement for the preserved Oshii Style lane.

Dungeon Style IS:

> **a controlled, graphic, high-contrast, low-noise, rust-red-accented industrial cel-illustration system optimized for the lived/maintained Second New Chongqing world and for stable downstream Seedance generation.**
